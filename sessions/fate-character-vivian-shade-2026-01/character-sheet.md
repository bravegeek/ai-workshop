## Description

Vivian Shade is a Memory Broker in the Sleeping City, trafficking in the most intimate currency of all: stolen dreams and memories. Born into a family of hereditary dreamwalkers, she learned the trade young alongside her cousin - their first shared dream led to their first theft, and from there, a thousand deals later, she built her reputation in the underground memory market.

But Viv made a critical mistake. She spied uninvited on a cursed woman's dreams - Evangeline Cross - and that violation created a psychic link. Now Eva unconsciously dreams through Viv's mind, slowly overwriting her identity from the inside out. The symptoms started mild - vertigo, disorientation - but have progressed to existential horror: Viv feels like a stranger in her own life, unable to recognize her own apartment, her own memories bleeding into Eva's until she can't tell which thoughts are hers.

The professional thief has become a desperate researcher, using every skill she possesses - breaking into minds, investigating leads, calling in favors from every dream scholar she's ever dealt with - to find a cure before there's nothing left of Vivian Shade. She sells stolen memories to fund her search, trading in the very thing she's losing. Meanwhile, her cousin takes extended walkabouts in the dream world, returning each time a little less normal, a little more lost - a mirror of Viv's possible future.

Viv is pragmatic, sharp-tongued, and increasingly desperate. She operates in the shadows of the Sleeping City where reality and dreams blur, carrying too many lives in her head, fighting to remember which one is hers.

## Aspects

### High Concept
**Memory Broker of the Sleeping City**

*Invoke this when: You're navigating the dream market, appraising memories, using your professional reputation, leveraging your expertise in memory theft, understanding how dreams work as commerce, calling on your experience in hundreds of minds, or using your broker network and knowledge.*

*Compel this when: Your profession puts you in danger (clients, cartels, rivals), you're recognized in the dream underworld at the worst time, professional obligations conflict with personal goals, the market comes calling with dangerous jobs, your reputation attracts unwanted attention, or the moral weight of trafficking in memories catches up to you.*

---

### Trouble
**Can't Remember Which Memories Are Mine**

*Invoke this when: You draw on experiences from stolen memories (you've "lived" many lives), recognize patterns from others' pasts, use empathy gained from carrying their memories, understand situations because someone else experienced them, or turn your contamination into insight.*

*Compel this when: You confuse someone else's memory for your own history, experience emotional reactions to events that didn't happen to you, false nostalgia or trauma triggers hit you, you make choices based on another's memories rather than your judgment, Eva's memories surface at the worst moment, or you're paralyzed by not knowing what's real.*

---

### Phase One: First Adventure
**One Stolen Dream, A Thousand Deals Later**

*Invoke this when: Your extensive experience in the memory trade gives you an edge, you've "seen it all" in a thousand deals, your long history provides contacts or knowledge, you reflect on how far you've come, or that first theft with your cousin taught you lessons you still use.*

*Compel this when: You can't remember which deal was which anymore (synergy with Trouble), your cousin remembers when it was just one stolen dream and judges how far you've fallen, you're haunted by the progression from one theft to a thousand, the first victim is still looking for what you took, you've lost perspective on the weight of what you do, or the sheer number of deals means someone from your past resurfaces with a problem.*

---

### Phase Two: Crossing Paths
**She's Dreaming Through Me Now**

*Invoke this when: You access Eva's knowledge or skills bleeding through, you understand her curse intimately, the psychic link gives you information about her, you can sense her dreams or state of mind, or her memories provide useful context.*

*Compel this when: Eva's dreams overwrite your thoughts, you act as she would rather than as yourself, her curse symptoms manifest in you (vertigo, disorientation), the link pulls you into her dreamscape involuntarily, you're haunted by her memories, the connection worsens under stress, someone mistakes you for her, or you're terrified you're being erased.*

---

### Phase Three: Current Situation
**Stranger in My Own Life, Selling Memories for Impossible Hope**

*Invoke this when: Alienation gives you objective perspective others lack, detachment allows ruthless decisions, you're motivated by desperate urgency, your professional resources fund the search for a cure, you have memories to trade for information, or your awareness of dissolution keeps you focused.*

*Compel this when: You don't recognize familiar places or people, your own apartment feels foreign, severe vertigo and disorientation strike at crucial moments, you're making dangerous deals for slim chances, doubt undermines your resolve ("impossible hope"), you're lost in your own neighborhood, the search feels increasingly futile, or despair creeps in and you keep going anyway because what else can you do?*

### Skill Descriptions

- **Burglary (+4)**: Viv's signature skill - breaking into sleeping minds like safes. She bypasses dream defenses, extracts memories cleanly, infiltrates protected dreamscapes, and navigates subconscious architecture with the precision of a master thief. When she's in your dream, you won't know she was there until she's already sold what she took.

- **Investigate (+3)**: Once just a thief, now a desperate detective. Viv follows leads obsessively, researches curses and dream mechanics, tracks memory trails through the market, and searches for the cure with single-minded intensity. Every clue about Eva's curse, every rumor of a severance ritual, every dream scholar's research - she pursues it all.

- **Contacts (+3)**: The Memory Broker knows everyone in the Sleeping City's dream trade. Buyers, sellers, scholars, curse-breakers, cartel enforcers, black market dealers - she's traded with them all. Now she's calling in every favor, burning through her network to find someone who can break the link before it's too late.

- **Empathy (+2)**: Memories carry emotions, and Viv reads them like text. She understands the psychological weight of what she steals, discerns the emotional content embedded in dreams, and navigates the intimate landscapes of others' minds. This skill helps her separate memories from dreams - and increasingly, helps her recognize when Eva's emotions are bleeding into her own.

- **Will (+2)**: Stubborn refusal to be erased. Viv fights Eva's overwriting with grim determination, resists mental attacks, maintains concentration despite vertigo and disorientation, and clings to her identity even as it dissolves. Not her strongest defense, but she's holding on.

- **Lore (+2)**: Not a scholar by training, but desperation makes fast students. Viv has thrown herself into research on curses, dreamwalking side effects, identity dissolution, and occult theory. She's learning what she needs to survive, even if the knowledge comes too slowly.

- **Stealth (+1)**: Basic thief training - moving unseen through dreamscapes, avoiding detection, slipping in and out of sleeping minds without leaving traces. Not her specialty, but foundational.

- **Deceive (+1)**: Professional necessity. Viv lies about what she steals, hides her deteriorating condition, manipulates clients and rivals, and covers her tracks in the memory market. She's good enough to get by.

- **Resources (+1)**: Memory brokering pays well when you're willing to traffic in dangerous goods. Viv has capital, stolen memories to trade, and access to the dream market's economy. She's converting everything into the search for a cure.

- **Notice (+1)**: Awareness in both waking and dreaming. Viv spots details others miss, notices when dream logic intrudes on reality (or vice versa), and stays alert despite the vertigo. Competent, but not exceptional.

## Stunts

### 1. Dreamspace Infiltrator
**Because I've broken into hundreds of sleeping minds**, when I use Burglary to infiltrate someone's dreamscape or extract a memory, I gain +2 to the roll.

*This is your bread and butter - what you were trained for, what you're best at. When you're doing professional memory theft, you're operating at +6 (Burglary +4, stunt +2). Use this for clean extractions, bypassing defenses, and navigating hostile dreamscapes. You're one of the best in the business.*

---

### 2. Every Dream Scholar Owes Me
**Because I've traded with every expert in the Sleeping City**, once per session I can declare that a dream scholar, curse-breaker, or occult researcher owes me a significant favor.

*When you're stuck and need expert help, invoke this. Introduce an NPC specialist who owes you from past deals. They must help, though how they help (and what complications arise) is up to the GM. Use this when you need crucial information about the curse, a ritual component, or access to forbidden knowledge. Once per session - make it count.*

---

### 3. Carrying Too Many Lives
**Because stolen memories have contaminated my identity**, once per session I can invoke a memory I've stolen (including Eva's) to gain +2 or a reroll on any action that memory's original owner would have been good at.

*Your Trouble becomes your strength. The memories bleeding into you include their skills and knowledge. Once per session, channel someone else's expertise - maybe Eva knows something useful, maybe a past theft victim had the skill you need now. Narratively describe whose memory you're drawing on and what they knew. Dangerous, intimate, and powerful. This is you using your dissolution as a weapon.*

## Backstory Summary

### The Family Gift and First Theft

Vivian Shade was born into a family of hereditary dreamwalkers - the ability runs in the blood. She received instruction from family members in navigating the dreamscape, discerning memories from dream logic, and walking the Sleeping City's surreal landscape. Her first shared dream was with her cousin, a formative experience that opened both their eyes to what dreamwalking could do.

Together, they got into mischief - and that mischief turned into their first memory theft. One stolen dream became a dozen, became a hundred, became a thousand deals. What started as curiosity and rebellion evolved into a profession. Viv became a Memory Broker, trafficking in the intimate currency of stolen dreams. Her cousin continued walking with her for a time, but now takes extended walkabouts in the dream world, returning each time a little less normal, a little more dissolved - a cautionary tale Viv sees in the mirror.

### The Curse of Evangeline Cross

Viv made a critical mistake. She spied uninvited on Evangeline Cross's dreams - a cursed woman whose dreamscape she infiltrated without permission. That violation, that trespass into a cursed mind, created a psychic link. Eva's curse recognized Viv, latched onto her, and now Eva unconsciously dreams through Viv's mind.

The side effects started mild: vertigo, disorientation, moments of confusion. But they've progressed into existential horror. Viv feels like a stranger in her own life. Her apartment doesn't feel like hers. Her memories blend with Eva's until she can't tell which thoughts, which emotions, which histories belong to Vivian Shade and which belong to Evangeline Cross. She's being slowly overwritten from the inside out, erased and replaced by Eva's dreaming consciousness.

Eva may not even know the link exists. She dreams, and those dreams flow through Viv, contaminating her identity, dissolving the boundaries of self.

### The Desperate Search

The professional thief has become a desperate researcher. Viv is using every skill she possesses - her thief expertise, her investigative instincts, her extensive network in the dream trade - to find a way to break the connection before she's completely erased.

She researches obsessively: curses, dream mechanics, identity dissolution, severance rituals. She calls in favors from every dream scholar, curse-breaker, and occult researcher she's ever dealt with. She sells stolen memories to fund her search, trading in the very thing she's losing, converting her professional capital into impossible hope.

The symptoms worsen. The vertigo strikes more frequently. The waking world feels less real every day. She's lost in familiar surroundings, alienated from her own existence. But she keeps searching, keeps fighting, keeps selling memories for slim chances and long-shot cures, because the alternative is dissolution into nothing - becoming Evangeline Cross's dream-puppet, a ghost haunting her own life.

## Notes & Reminders

- **Dreamwalking Mechanics**: Memories are embedded in dreams. A dreamwalker must discern the true memory from the dream's symbolic/surreal wrapper. This requires skill and experience.

- **The Dreamwalking Spectrum**: Document the five stages of dreamwalking degradation for potential character progression:
  1. **Early Stage**: "The Lines Are Starting to Blur" - Catching early symptoms, reality feels slightly off, manageable but worsening
  2. **Middle Stage**: "Can't Remember Which Memories Are Mine" - **(Viv's current Trouble)** - Identity contamination, confusion between stolen and personal memories
  3. **Advanced Stage**: "Dissolving Between the Waking and the Dream" - Phasing between realities, becoming something neither fully real nor dream
  4. **Critical Stage**: "Forgetting How to Wake Up" - Spending more time in dreams, waking world feels less real, choosing dreams over reality
  5. **Terminal Stage**: "There's More Dream Than Person Left" - Barely human, existing in both worlds, point of no return

- **Phase Two Progression**: Viv's relationship with Eva follows a cause-and-effect arc:
  - **Stage 1 (Betrayal)**: Viv spied uninvited on Eva's cursed dreams
  - **Stage 2 (Consequence)**: The curse created a link - Eva now dreams through Viv unconsciously
  - **Current State**: Viv is being overwritten, desperately searching for a way to sever the connection

- **Phase Three Progression**: Viv's emotional journey through the search:
  - **Stage 1 (Recognition)**: Existential horror - feeling like a stranger in her own life, fighting the dissolution
  - **Stage 2 (Desperate Action)**: Selling memories to fund the search, calling in every favor
  - **Stage 3 (Creeping Despair)**: Growing doubt ("impossible hope"), questioning if there's even a cure, but searching anyway

- **Cousin as Cautionary Tale**: Viv's cousin is further along the dreamwalking degradation spectrum (possibly Advanced or Critical stage). She takes extended walkabouts in the dream world and returns each time less "normal" - a living warning of what happens when you go too deep.

- **Eva's Curse Symptoms in Viv**:
  - **Mild**: Vertigo, disorientation, physical instability
  - **Severe**: Feeling lost in familiar surroundings, not recognizing your own life/world, existential alienation, being a stranger in your own reality

- **Burglary as Dream Skill**: Viv's Burglary +4 represents breaking into minds, bypassing mental defenses, extracting memories, and navigating dreamscape architecture - not physical lock-picking.

- **Stunt Synergies**:
  - Use **Dreamspace Infiltrator** for professional memory theft (+6 total with Burglary +4)
  - Use **Every Dream Scholar Owes Me** when stuck on curse research (once per session)
  - Use **Carrying Too Many Lives** to channel stolen memories' skills (once per session) - includes Eva's abilities

- **Fate Point Economy**:
  - Spend on: Critical Burglary rolls for memory theft, desperate Investigate rolls for cure leads, invoking aspects when fighting Eva's influence
  - Gain from: Accept compels on Trouble (identity confusion), Phase Two (Eva's dreams overtaking you), Phase Three (despair and alienation)

## Play Styles

Vivian Shade can be played in multiple ways depending on what aspects of her concept you emphasize. Here are 5 distinct approaches:

### Style 1: The Professional Under Pressure (Competence)

**Core Approach**: Emphasize Viv's expertise and professionalism despite her deteriorating condition - she's a master thief fighting to stay functional.

**Tactics**:
- Lead with Burglary +4 and **Dreamspace Infiltrator** for +6 total on memory theft - you're exceptional at your job
- Use **Memory Broker of the Sleeping City** to navigate the dream market with authority and reputation
- Invoke Contacts +3 to leverage your network - you know everyone and they know you're good
- Stay focused on professional competence: clean extractions, smart trades, maintaining reputation
- Accept compels on Phase Three to show the cracks, but bounce back with determination

**Fate Point Economy**:
- **Spend on**: Critical Burglary rolls to maintain your reputation, Contacts rolls to preserve relationships
- **Gain from**: Accept compels on **Stranger in My Own Life** when the symptoms show, but push through professionally

**Strengths**: High reliability, excellent at core function, maintains respect in the dream trade
**Weaknesses**: Denial of how bad things are getting, relying on competence when you need help

---

### Style 2: The Desperate Detective (Investigation)

**Core Approach**: Prioritize the search for a cure above all else - every action serves finding the answer.

**Tactics**:
- Use Investigate +3 constantly - researching curses, following leads, tracking down information
- Invoke **Every Dream Scholar Owes Me** aggressively - call in that favor when you need expert help
- Invoke **One Stolen Dream, A Thousand Deals Later** for your extensive experience and pattern recognition
- Use **Stranger in My Own Life** for the urgent motivation - you're racing against dissolution
- Combine Investigate with Lore +2 for research montages and following academic trails

**Fate Point Economy**:
- **Spend on**: Investigate rolls for crucial breakthroughs, invoking aspects to stay focused on the cure
- **Gain from**: Accept compels that distract you from the search, forcing you to waste time on market business

**Strengths**: Focused, driven, excellent at gathering information and solving mysteries
**Weaknesses**: Tunnel vision, ignoring other priorities, burning bridges in pursuit of the cure

---

### Style 3: The Identity-Haunted Survivor (Psychological Horror)

**Core Approach**: Lean into the horror of dissolution - emphasize the existential terror and struggle to stay yourself.

**Tactics**:
- Accept compels on **Can't Remember Which Memories Are Mine** and **She's Dreaming Through Me Now** frequently for Fate Points
- Use Will +2 to resist mental attacks and Eva's influence - you're holding on by your fingernails
- Invoke **Carrying Too Many Lives** to show the contamination working both ways - using Eva's (or others') skills
- Play up the symptoms: vertigo, disorientation, alienation, confusion between self and other
- Use Empathy +2 to understand what's happening to you psychologically, even if you can't stop it

**Fate Point Economy**:
- **Spend on**: Will rolls to resist Eva, critical moments of asserting "this is who I am"
- **Gain from**: Accept every identity-confusion compel, lean into the horror for maximum Fate Points

**Strengths**: Emotionally powerful, narratively compelling, generates lots of Fate Points
**Weaknesses**: Vulnerable, unreliable, may accelerate your own dissolution

---

### Style 4: The Networked Dealer (Social Manipulation)

**Core Approach**: Use your relationships and the memory market to solve problems - you're a broker first, everything else second.

**Tactics**:
- Lead with Contacts +3 - you know someone who can help with everything
- Use **Memory Broker of the Sleeping City** to leverage reputation and professional standing
- Invoke **Every Dream Scholar Owes Me** strategically - that once-per-session favor is gold
- Trade memories (Resources +1) for information, favors, and access - everything is transactional
- Use Deceive +1 and Empathy +2 for social manipulation - reading people and lying when necessary

**Fate Point Economy**:
- **Spend on**: Contacts and Rapport rolls to build/maintain relationships, social manipulation
- **Gain from**: Accept compels when your network demands reciprocity at bad times

**Strengths**: Well-connected, resourceful, can call on help from many sources
**Weaknesses**: Dependent on others, burning social capital, may owe more than you can pay

---

### Style 5: The Contaminated Weapon (Turning Weakness to Strength)

**Core Approach**: Embrace the contamination tactically - you carry too many lives, so use them all.

**Tactics**:
- Invoke **Can't Remember Which Memories Are Mine** for the positive side - you've lived many lives and learned from them
- Use **Carrying Too Many Lives** aggressively - once per session, channel whoever's skills you need
- Invoke **One Stolen Dream, A Thousand Deals Later** for vast experience across countless stolen memories
- Play Viv as someone who's accepted she's plural now and weaponizes it
- Use Empathy +2 to understand which memories to draw on and when

**Fate Point Economy**:
- **Spend on**: **Carrying Too Many Lives** stunt, invoking Trouble for the skills you've absorbed
- **Gain from**: Accept the downsides of contamination, but frame them as the price of power

**Strengths**: Versatile, surprising, narratively interesting, turns curse into capability
**Weaknesses**: Accelerating dissolution, losing your core identity, becoming a collection of fragments

### Universal Tactics (All Styles)

**Aspect Invocation Priority**:
1. **Memory Broker of the Sleeping City** - Your most versatile aspect, use for professional competence, market knowledge, and dreamwalking expertise
2. **Stranger in My Own Life, Selling Memories for Impossible Hope** - Use for desperate motivation, funding the search, or detachment giving clarity
3. **One Stolen Dream, A Thousand Deals Later** - Use for experience, contacts from past deals, or remembering old lessons

**Compel Management**:
- **Accept compels on Trouble and Phase Two frequently** - identity confusion and Eva's influence generate great drama and Fate Points
- **Negotiate compels on Phase Three** - "impossible hope" leading to despair can be session-defining
- **Refuse compels when asserting identity** - sometimes you NEED to know who you are, even if it costs a Fate Point

**Creating Advantages**:
Use your high skills to create stackable Aspects, then unleash 2-3 free invokes on one massive roll:
- **Burglary (+4)**: "Mapped Their Dreamscape", "Found the Hidden Memory", "Bypassed Their Defenses"
- **Investigate (+3)**: "Uncovered the Curse's Origin", "Connected the Dots", "Paper Trail to the Culprit"
- **Contacts (+3)**: "Called In Multiple Favors", "The Market Owes Me", "Dream Scholar Alliance"
- **Empathy (+2)**: "Read Their Emotional State", "Understood Their Fear", "Sensed the Truth"

**Stress Management**:
- **Physical Stress**: 2 boxes - You're not a fighter. Avoid physical conflicts. Use Stealth +1 to escape.
- **Mental Stress**: 3 boxes - More resilient mentally, but not exceptional. Use Will +2 to defend, but don't tank mental attacks.
- **Use Consequences narratively**: Mental consequences tie beautifully to your Trouble - take "Eva's Memories Surfacing" or "Lost in My Own Apartment" as Mild consequences

**Recommended Starting Style**: Try **Style 2 (Desperate Detective)** for your first session - it gives you clear goals (find the cure), uses your strong skills (Investigate +3, Contacts +3), and lets you invoke **Every Dream Scholar Owes Me** to get unstuck. As you get comfortable, blend styles based on the situation: investigate when searching, go professional when working, lean into horror during downtime.

**Most Important**: Fate rewards engaging with your Aspects. Accept compels on your Trouble and Phase Two regularly - the identity confusion is the heart of Viv's tragedy. Generate Fate Points through accepting the horror, then spend them on the desperate moments where you assert who you are and fight to save yourself. Your story is about dissolution and defiance - lean into both.

## Character Development Ideas

- Will Viv find a way to break the connection with Eva before complete dissolution? Or will she discover the only way to survive is to integrate Eva's identity with her own?
- What is the true nature of Eva's curse? Who cursed her, and why? Is breaking the link even possible without understanding the curse's origin?
- Viv's cousin is further along the degradation spectrum - could she hold the key to survival? Or is she a warning of the inevitable end?
- What if Eva discovers the link? Would she be horrified? Relieved (is Viv carrying her curse)? Angry at the violation?
- As Viv uses **Carrying Too Many Lives**, is she becoming stronger or losing herself faster? At what point does tactical use of contamination accelerate her doom?
- The memory market operates in moral gray areas - what memories has Viv sold that she regrets? Are any of her past victims hunting her?
- What would Viv sacrifice to break the link? Her career? Her family? Someone else's life?
- Is there a Memory Cartel that wants Viv working for them? What happens if they learn about her vulnerability?
