## Session Summary

Created a Memory Broker character for a Surreal Dream Noir setting - a world where reality and dreams blur in classic noir style. The character evolved through collaborative worldbuilding and narrative choices that created rich thematic progressions.

## Design Choices & Rationale

### Setting Selection
- Chosen: **Surreal Dream Noir** (Option C from initial offerings)
- Influences: Dark City, Inception, Paprika with noir detective aesthetics
- Perfect fit for dreamwalking themes with psychological horror and hard-boiled drama

### High Concept Development
- Final: **Memory Broker of the Sleeping City**
- Chosen from "dream thief" variations, specifically Option E
- Emphasizes: Business/commerce angle, noir underworld, dreams as currency
- Corporate noir meets dream trade - memories as commodities

### Trouble Development: The Spectrum Approach
- User's brilliant insight: Dreamwalking consequences exist on a **spectrum of degradation**
- Created five stages from early symptoms to terminal dissolution
- Final Trouble: **Can't Remember Which Memories Are Mine** (Middle Stage)
- Allows for character progression/deterioration throughout campaign
- All five stages documented in character sheet for reference

### Aspect Progressions
The character features **two major narrative arcs** built into aspects:

**Phase Two Progression (C→F)**:
- Stage 1: She hired you to remove a memory, you kept a copy (betrayal)
- Stage 2: That memory has taken over part of your identity (consequence)
- Final Aspect: **She's Dreaming Through Me Now**
- Arc: Professional betrayal → Personal horror

**Phase Three Progression (G→I)**:
- Stage 1: Existential horror - feeling like a stranger in your own life
- Stage 2: Desperate action - selling memories to fund the search
- Stage 3: Creeping despair - "impossible hope" doubt
- Final Aspect: **Stranger in My Own Life, Selling Memories for Impossible Hope**
- Arc: Recognition → Desperate action → Creeping despair

Both progressions documented in character sheet for narrative development.

### Worldbuilding Contributions

**Hereditary Dreamwalking**:
- Dreamwalking is a familial trait, runs in bloodlines
- Viv's family provides instruction in the gift
- Creates family drama potential and legacy themes

**Memories Embedded in Dreams**:
- Technical detail: Memories exist within dreams
- Dreamwalkers must discern true memory from dream's symbolic wrapper
- Adds skill requirement and complexity to memory theft

**The Cursed Victim Dynamic**:
- User shifted from "hired to remove memory" to "spied on cursed person"
- Creates violation/trespass angle rather than transactional
- Curse spreads to those who touch it - adds supernatural danger
- Eva doesn't necessarily know about the link (asymmetric relationship)

**Curse Symptoms**:
- Mild: Vertigo, disorientation (physical)
- Severe: Feeling lost in familiar surroundings, existential alienation
- Progression: Physical → Existential → Identity dissolution

**The Cousin as Cautionary Tale**:
- Another family member, also dreamwalks
- Takes extended "walkabouts" in dream world
- Returns progressively less "normal" each time
- Further along degradation spectrum than Viv
- Living warning of what happens when you go too deep

### Character Evolution: Thief → Researcher
- User's key insight: Viv **started as a thief** but is **now searching for cure**
- Required hybrid skill pyramid reflecting both training and desperate adaptation
- Skills show evolution: Burglary foundation + developed Investigation/Contacts
- Final pyramid: "Balanced Desperate" - every skill bent toward survival

### Names
- **Vivian "Viv" Shade**: Classic noir, "vivid" dreams + "shade" shadow work
- **Evangeline "Eva" Cross**: Tragic, angelic name + heavy surname
- Both have nicknames for intimacy/casual reference

### Mechanical Choices

**Skills** (Balanced Desperate package):
- Great (+4): Burglary - thief foundation, still her best
- Good (+3): Investigate, Contacts - desperate researcher developments
- Fair (+2): Empathy, Will, Lore - supporting competencies
- Average (+1): Stealth, Deceive, Resources, Notice - rounded basics

**Stunts** (Thematic Powerhouse package):
1. **Dreamspace Infiltrator** - Professional excellence (+2 Burglary for memory theft)
2. **Every Dream Scholar Owes Me** - Phase Three callback (once per session favor)
3. **Carrying Too Many Lives** - Trouble synergy (use stolen memories' skills)

Refresh: 3 (standard)

### Narrative Themes
- **Identity dissolution**: Core horror - who are you when memories aren't yours?
- **Profession as curse**: The memory broker is losing memories
- **Desperate evolution**: Thief forced to become detective/researcher
- **Impossible hope**: Searching despite growing doubt
- **Contamination as weapon**: Turning dissolution into tactical advantage
- **Family legacy**: Gift becomes curse across generations
- **Noir fatalism**: The inevitable downward spiral with defiant struggle

## Play Style Recommendations

Created 5 distinct play styles:
1. **Professional Under Pressure** - Competence despite deterioration
2. **Desperate Detective** - Investigation-focused (recommended for first session)
3. **Identity-Haunted Survivor** - Psychological horror emphasis
4. **Networked Dealer** - Social manipulation and market focus
5. **Contaminated Weapon** - Turning weakness to strength

Recommended starting with Style 2 (Desperate Detective) for clear goals and strong skill use, then blending based on situation.

## Character Arc Potential

**Short-term**: Finding leads on curse-breaking, managing symptoms, maintaining professional reputation while deteriorating

**Medium-term**: Discovering Eva's curse origin, confronting the cousin's decline, moral compromises for slim chances at cure

**Long-term**:
- Break the link and survive (happy ending?)
- Integrate Eva's identity (transformation)
- Complete dissolution (tragedy)
- Discover cure requires terrible sacrifice
- Progress to next stage on degradation spectrum

**Key Questions**:
- What is Eva's curse and who inflicted it?
- Does Eva know about the link?
- Can the cousin be saved, or is she beyond help?
- What price is Viv willing to pay for survival?
- At what point does using contamination tactically accelerate doom?
