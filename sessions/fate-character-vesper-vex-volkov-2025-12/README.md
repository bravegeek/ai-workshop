## The Concept

A street graffiti artist who accidentally activated ancient symbol magic and learned insect-enhanced sorcery from roach spirits. She's caught between her loyal but terrified crew, the law, and a sinister Moth Cult that wants to recruit her.

She paints with living ink. Her symbols crawl.

---

## Playing Vesper

**She Excels At**:
- Creating powerful magical symbols in urban spaces (+6 Crafts)
- Parkour and urban navigation (Athletics +3)
- Stealth, infiltration, and breaking into places (Stealth +2, Burglary +2)
- Occult research and insect lore (Lore +3)

**She Struggles With**:
- Controlling the swarm (Will +1 - narratively intentional!)
- Direct combat (Fight +1)
- Authority and formal social situations
- Choosing between her old life and her power

**Tactical Notes**:
- Use environment creatively (urban terrain is your canvas)
- Invoke Aspects aggressively to fuel Fate Point economy
- Accept compels on "The Swarm Hungers" for dramatic moments
- Living Ink is versatile - insects can scout, distract, carry, attack, etc.
- Roach-Whispered Secrets once per session for crucial info

## Play Styles

Vesper can be played in multiple ways depending on how you leverage her abilities:

### 1. The Urban Ritual Mage (Preparation & Control)
- **Core**: Prepare battlefield with pre-painted symbols before conflict
- **Best for**: Strategic gameplay, territorial defense
- Paint symbols at +6 throughout your territory, activate with Living Ink when needed
- **Trade-off**: Requires prep time, vulnerable when ambushed

### 2. The Hit-and-Run Tagger (Mobility & Chaos)
- **Core**: Strike fast, leave magical graffiti, vanish with parkour
- **Best for**: Action scenes, high mobility gameplay
- Quick tags mid-chase, Athletics +3 escapes, Living Ink distractions
- **Trade-off**: Less control, can't afford direct combat

### 3. The Information Broker (Investigation & Leverage)
- **Core**: Use insect surveillance and Roach-Whispered Secrets for intel
- **Best for**: Investigation campaigns, political intrigue
- Once-per-session knowledge reveals, insect scouts for eavesdropping, Lore +3 research
- **Trade-off**: Once-per-session limit, needs investigative hooks

### 4. The Chaos Artist (Embracing the Swarm's Hunger)
- **Core**: Accept EVERY compel on "The Swarm Hungers" to stockpile fate points
- **Best for**: High-risk dramatic moments, memorable chaos
- Generate 2-3+ fate points per session, spend explosively on massive effects
- **Trade-off**: Very unpredictable, can derail plans

### 5. The Social Manipulator (Double Life Balance)
- **Core**: Navigate between mundane and magical worlds using relationships
- **Best for**: Social intrigue, relationship-heavy campaigns
- Maintain street connections, blame Moths for your actions, create social advantages
- **Trade-off**: Lower social skills need fate point support

### 6. The Reluctant Cultist (Playing Both Sides)
- **Core**: Engage directly with Moth Cultists—infiltrate, manipulate, or consider their offer
- **Best for**: Moral complexity, character development arcs
- Leverage their interest in you, research moth magic, experiment with hybrid symbols
- **Trade-off**: Risky (might get corrupted), strains crew relationships

### Universal Tactics

**Aspect Invocation Priority**:
1. Free invokes on Aspects you created
2. "Graffiti Mage Who Paints with Living Ink" on Crafts rolls
3. Multiple Aspects on critical rolls

**Creating Advantages** - Stack 2-3 free invokes, then unleash on one massive roll:
- **Crafts +4**: "Protective Ward," "Glyph of Confusion"
- **Lore +3**: "I Know Their Weakness"
- **Athletics +3**: "High Ground," "Escape Route"

**Stress Management**: Only 2 Physical/3 Mental stress boxes—AVOID direct conflict. Use Consequences narratively: "Roaches Feeding Frenzy (Mild)" creates story hooks.

**Recommended Start**: Try Style 1 or 2 first (Urban Ritual or Hit-and-Run)—both leverage your best skills and feel immediately powerful. Blend styles as you get comfortable.

**Most Important**: Vesper's Aspects are double-edged by design. The most fun play style engages your Aspects aggressively—both invoking AND accepting compels. She's built for dramatic tension. Lean into it.
