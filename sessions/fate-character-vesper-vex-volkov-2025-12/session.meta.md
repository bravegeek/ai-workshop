## Character Concept

A female graffiti artist who accidentally discovered symbol-based magic and uses insects to enhance it. Started as a skilled street artist and urban athlete before stumbling into the mystical world.

---

## Design Choices & Reasoning

### Aspects
1. **High Concept - "Graffiti Mage Who Paints with Living Ink"**: Captures the artistic origin and the unique insect-enhanced magic system in one evocative phrase
2. **Trouble - "The Swarm Hungers for More Than Magic"**: Creates mechanical tension (with low Will) and narrative complications
3. **Phase One - "The Roaches Answered When I Opened the Wrong Door"**: Two-part origin (accidental activation → insect mentorship) provides rich backstory hooks
4. **Phase Two - "My Crew Still Has My Back (Even If They're Terrified)"**: Grounds the character in humanity and creates relationship drama
5. **Phase Three - "The Moths Keep Leaving Me... Invitations"**: Active antagonist/recruitment subplot for ongoing campaign tension

### Skills
- **Crafts at +4**: Core competency as artist-mage; with Street Canvas Mastery, reaches +6 in urban settings
- **Lore & Athletics at +3**: Reflects her dual nature—occult knowledge and physical prowess from her graffiti days
- **Burglary, Notice, Stealth at +2**: Urban infiltration suite; essential for illegal tagging and magical activities
- **Will at +1**: Deliberately low to create mechanical risk when controlling the swarm; turns Trouble into real gameplay challenge

### Stunts
1. **Living Ink**: Signature ability; versatile insect utility tied to artistic magic
2. **Street Canvas Mastery**: Domain expertise; makes her exceptional in urban environments (+6 total)
3. **Roach-Whispered Secrets**: Information-gathering tied to origin; once-per-session balances power

## Narrative Hooks

- The nature of the "wrong door" she opened
- The Moth Cultists' true agenda and what joining would mean
- Her crew's breaking point—when does fear overcome loyalty?
- The swarm's ultimate purpose or master (if any)
- Other practitioners of symbol magic—allies or rivals?
- What happened to the warehouse? Is it still dangerous?

---

## Gameplay Notes

- Character excels at: Urban magical crafting, parkour/infiltration, occult research
- Character struggles with: Swarm control (low Will), social situations requiring authority, direct combat
- Fate Point economy: Encourage aggressive invoking of Aspects; compels on Trouble and Phase Three will generate points
- Combat style: Likely uses environment, created symbols, and insect swarms rather than direct fighting (Fight +1)
