## Description

Vesper "Vex" Volkov is a talented graffiti artist who stumbled into the world of symbol magic when one of her warehouse tags accidentally opened a mystical gateway. The roaches that emerged became her teachers, guiding her through the ancient art of insect-enhanced glyph magic. Now she walks a dangerous line between her old crew (who fear what she's become), street life, the authorities, and the sinister Moth Cultists who want to recruit her into their hive.

She paints with living ink—her symbols crawl and swarm with purpose. Her crew still calls her "Vex," the sharp, quick tag name she earned on the streets before the magic came.

## Stunts

### 1. Living Ink
**Because you Paint with Living Ink**, when you use **Crafts** to create a magical symbol, you can spend a fate point to have insects emerge from the paint to perform one specific task (carry messages, scout an area, distract enemies, form a bridge, etc.). The effect lasts for one scene.

*This is your signature ability—versatile insect utility powered by your art.*

---

### 2. Street Canvas Mastery
**Because you're a Graffiti Mage**, you get **+2 to Crafts** when creating magical symbols in urban environments (walls, trains, tunnels, buildings). Additionally, symbols you create in your territory are particularly potent.

*In your element, you're operating at Superb (+6) Crafts—truly exceptional.*

---

### 3. Roach-Whispered Secrets
**Because The Roaches Answered When You Opened the Wrong Door**, once per session you can call upon the swarm to reveal hidden knowledge. Make a **Lore** roll to learn one piece of occult information, the location of a hidden thing, or overhear a recent conversation—the insects were there, and they remember.

*Your origin gives you access to impossible information—the roaches see everything.*

## Backstory Summary

### The Accidental Awakening
Vesper "Vex" Volkov was a skilled graffiti artist and urban athlete, a cornerstone member of her crew known for incredible tagging skills and parkour prowess. During a late-night mission to tag an abandoned warehouse, she spray-painted symbols she'd seen in dreams. The tag activated something ancient—a doorway, a ward, a summoning. Roaches poured forth, but instead of fleeing, she listened. They spoke in chittering whispers, teaching her the language of living glyphs.

### The Growing Divide
As her powers grew, so did the distance between her and her crew. They still love her, still have her back, but they're terrified of what she's become. Meanwhile, the Moth Cultists—a rival insect-magic faction—have taken notice. They leave invitations written in dust and wings, promising power and belonging. She hasn't accepted... yet.

### The Hunger
The swarm isn't just a tool. It's alive, with needs and desires. Some days she controls it. Other days, it controls her. With her limited Will, keeping the insects in check is an ongoing struggle—and that makes every spell a risk.

## Notes & Reminders

- **Low Will = Narrative Gold**: Your +1 Will means controlling the swarm is genuinely difficult. Embrace the chaos!
- **Urban Dominance**: In the city, creating symbols at +6 (Crafts +4, Street Canvas Mastery +2) makes you formidable
- **Roach-Whispered Secrets**: Remember you can use this once per session for crucial information
- **Living Ink versatility**: Get creative with insect tasks—they're limited only by imagination and GM approval
- **Fate Point Economy**: Invoke your Aspects aggressively, and don't be afraid to accept compels for more Fate Points
