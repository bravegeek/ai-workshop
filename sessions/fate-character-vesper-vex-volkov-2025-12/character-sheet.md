## Description

Vesper "Vex" Volkov is a talented graffiti artist who stumbled into the world of symbol magic when one of her warehouse tags accidentally opened a mystical gateway. The roaches that emerged became her teachers, guiding her through the ancient art of insect-enhanced glyph magic. Now she walks a dangerous line between her old crew (who fear what she's become), street life, the authorities, and the sinister Moth Cultists who want to recruit her into their hive.

She paints with living ink—her symbols crawl and swarm with purpose. Her crew still calls her "Vex," the sharp, quick tag name she earned on the streets before the magic came.

## Aspects

### High Concept
**Graffiti Mage Who Paints with Living Ink**

*A street artist who discovered symbol magic and enhances it with insect swarms. Invokable for artistic magic, urban navigation, and insect control. Compellable when her art draws unwanted attention or the insects get out of hand.*

### Trouble
**The Swarm Hungers for More Than Magic**

*The insects she uses have their own needs and desires. Invokable when the swarm's instincts align with her goals. Compellable when they act independently, demand feeding, or spiral out of her control (especially with her modest Will).*

### Phase One: Your First Adventure
**The Roaches Answered When I Opened the Wrong Door**

*Her accidental activation of ancient wards led to an alliance with roach spirits who taught her the first symbols. Invokable for insect communication, occult lore, or when dealing with dark/forbidden magic. Compellable when the consequences of that "wrong door" return, or when the roaches demand payment for their teaching.*

### Phase Two: Crossing Paths
**My Crew Still Has My Back (Even If They're Terrified)**

*Her old graffiti artist friends remain loyal despite their fear of her new powers. Invokable for street contacts, alibis, backup, or safe houses. Compellable when their fear causes problems, when protecting them puts her at risk, or when they beg her to stop.*

### Phase Three: Crossing Paths Again
**The Moths Keep Leaving Me... Invitations**

*A disturbing insect-magic cult actively recruits her, leaving cryptic messages and "gifts." Invokable when she needs information about rival insect magic or wants to infiltrate/manipulate the cult. Compellable when the Moths interfere, stalk her, threaten those she cares about, or when their invitations become impossible to ignore.*

## Stunts

### 1. Living Ink
**Because you Paint with Living Ink**, when you use **Crafts** to create a magical symbol, you can spend a fate point to have insects emerge from the paint to perform one specific task (carry messages, scout an area, distract enemies, form a bridge, etc.). The effect lasts for one scene.

*This is your signature ability—versatile insect utility powered by your art.*

---

### 2. Street Canvas Mastery
**Because you're a Graffiti Mage**, you get **+2 to Crafts** when creating magical symbols in urban environments (walls, trains, tunnels, buildings). Additionally, symbols you create in your territory are particularly potent.

*In your element, you're operating at Superb (+6) Crafts—truly exceptional.*

---

### 3. Roach-Whispered Secrets
**Because The Roaches Answered When You Opened the Wrong Door**, once per session you can call upon the swarm to reveal hidden knowledge. Make a **Lore** roll to learn one piece of occult information, the location of a hidden thing, or overhear a recent conversation—the insects were there, and they remember.

*Your origin gives you access to impossible information—the roaches see everything.*

## Backstory Summary

### The Accidental Awakening
Vesper "Vex" Volkov was a skilled graffiti artist and urban athlete, a cornerstone member of her crew known for incredible tagging skills and parkour prowess. During a late-night mission to tag an abandoned warehouse, she spray-painted symbols she'd seen in dreams. The tag activated something ancient—a doorway, a ward, a summoning. Roaches poured forth, but instead of fleeing, she listened. They spoke in chittering whispers, teaching her the language of living glyphs.

### The Growing Divide
As her powers grew, so did the distance between her and her crew. They still love her, still have her back, but they're terrified of what she's become. Meanwhile, the Moth Cultists—a rival insect-magic faction—have taken notice. They leave invitations written in dust and wings, promising power and belonging. She hasn't accepted... yet.

### The Hunger
The swarm isn't just a tool. It's alive, with needs and desires. Some days she controls it. Other days, it controls her. With her limited Will, keeping the insects in check is an ongoing struggle—and that makes every spell a risk.

## Play Styles

Vesper can be played in multiple ways depending on what aspects of her concept you emphasize. Here are six distinct approaches:

### Style 1: The Urban Ritual Mage (Preparation & Control)

**Core Approach**: Prepare the battlefield with pre-painted symbols before conflict begins.

**Tactics**:
- Use downtime to create symbols throughout your territory at +6 Crafts
- Paint protective wards, traps, and summoning circles in advance
- When trouble arrives, activate pre-painted symbols with Living Ink for instant swarm deployment
- Invoke "My Crew Still Has My Back" for advance warning of danger

**Fate Point Economy**:
- **Spend on**: Living Ink activations, boosting Crafts rolls for complex rituals
- **Gain from**: Compels on "The Moths Keep Leaving Me Invitations" (interference), "The Swarm Hungers" (premature activation)

**Strengths**: Maximizes your +6 Crafts, turns territory into a fortress, strategic gameplay
**Weaknesses**: Requires prep time, vulnerable when ambushed or outside territory, unpredictable with low Will

---

### Style 2: The Hit-and-Run Tagger (Mobility & Chaos)

**Core Approach**: Use Athletics and Stealth to strike fast, leave magical graffiti, and vanish.

**Tactics**:
- Create Advantages with Burglary to reach impossible locations
- Quick tags with Crafts mid-chase or mid-conflict
- Athletics +3 parkour escapes while symbols activate behind you
- Living Ink creates swarm distractions to cover your retreat

**Fate Point Economy**:
- **Spend on**: Athletics escapes, Stealth to vanish, quick Crafts under pressure
- **Gain from**: Compels on "My Crew Still Has My Back (Even If They're Terrified)" when hiding with them risks them

**Strengths**: High mobility, hard to pin down, dramatic action scenes
**Weaknesses**: Less control over outcomes, relies on fate points, can't afford direct combat

---

### Style 3: The Information Broker (Investigation & Leverage)

**Core Approach**: Use Roach-Whispered Secrets and insect surveillance to gather intelligence and manipulate situations.

**Tactics**:
- Roach-Whispered Secrets once per session for crucial information at the perfect moment
- Create Advantages with Notice to spot clues, surveillance, magical signs
- Invoke "My Crew Still Has My Back" to access street networks
- Lore +3 for researching symbols, occult factions, enemy weaknesses
- Send Living Ink insect scouts for passive surveillance and eavesdropping

**Fate Point Economy**:
- **Spend on**: Lore research rolls, Notice for crucial details, Living Ink reconnaissance
- **Gain from**: Compels on "The Roaches Answered When I Opened the Wrong Door" (they demand payment for info), "The Moths Keep Leaving Me Invitations" (their messages contain clues you can't ignore)

**Strengths**: Knowledge is power in Fate, creates narrative control, Roach-Whispered Secrets is a trump card
**Weaknesses**: Once-per-session limit on signature ability, needs investigative hooks, less effective in combat

---

### Style 4: The Chaos Artist (Embracing the Swarm's Hunger)

**Core Approach**: Accept compels on "The Swarm Hungers for More Than Magic" frequently to generate fate points, then spend them explosively.

**Tactics**:
- Accept EVERY compel to generate 2-3+ fate points per session
- Build massive fate point pool, then spend aggressively on Living Ink and multiple Aspect invokes
- Low Will is a feature—lean into the unpredictability
- Use swarms to create distractions, panic, and widespread confusion

**Fate Point Economy**:
- **Spend on**: Multiple invokes on critical rolls, Living Ink spam, dramatic overcomes
- **Gain from**: Compels on "The Swarm Hungers" (constantly), "The Roaches Answered When I Opened the Wrong Door" (their own agenda), "My Crew Still Has My Back" (protecting them backfires)

**Strengths**: Generates massive fate point pools, creates unforgettable chaotic scenes, high-risk/high-reward, leans into narrative weakness
**Weaknesses**: Very unpredictable outcomes, can derail plans, may damage ally relationships, requires GM who enjoys chaos

---

### Style 5: The Social Manipulator (Double Life Balance)

**Core Approach**: Use Aspects to navigate between mundane and magical worlds, leveraging relationships.

**Tactics**:
- Rapport +1 + Aspects: Invoke "My Crew Still Has My Back" to maintain street connections
- Contacts +1 to build network of informants, allies, and dupes
- Deception through misdirection: Use "The Moths Keep Leaving Me Invitations" to blame them for your actions
- Create social Advantages: "They Trust the Old Vex," "Gang Thinks I'm Good Luck," "Cop Owes Me a Favor"

**Fate Point Economy**:
- **Spend on**: Rapport to build trust, Contacts to find the right person, Crafts to create "gifts" (magical bribes)
- **Gain from**: Compels on "My Crew Still Has My Back (Even If They're Terrified)" when maintaining relationships costs you, "The Moths Keep Leaving Me Invitations" when they expose you socially

**Strengths**: Maintains connections to humanity, creates complex social webs, Fate loves relationship drama
**Weaknesses**: Lower social skills (+1) require fate point support, lies eventually unravel, needs GM engagement with NPCs

---

### Style 6: The Reluctant Cultist (Playing Both Sides)

**Core Approach**: Engage with the Moth Cultists directly—infiltrate, manipulate, or genuinely consider their offer.

**Tactics**:
- Invoke "The Moths Keep Leaving Me Invitations"—they WANT you, use that leverage
- Lore +3 to research their magic and understand (or counter) them
- Create Advantages: "They Think I'm Interested," "I Know Their Secret Rituals," "The Moths Trust Me"
- Experiment with moth-roach hybrid symbols (describe creatively)

**Fate Point Economy**:
- **Spend on**: Lore rolls to understand moth magic, Crafts for hybrid symbols, Rapport/Deceive to convince them
- **Gain from**: Compels on "The Moths Keep Leaving Me Invitations" (they demand more commitment), "My Crew Still Has My Back" (they're horrified), "The Roaches Answered When I Opened the Wrong Door" (roaches jealous of moths)

**Strengths**: Deep engagement with antagonists, moral complexity, character development, potential access to moth magic
**Weaknesses**: Risky (might get recruited/corrupted), crew relationships suffer, requires fleshed-out cultists, no mechanical support

### Universal Tactics (All Styles)

**Aspect Invocation Priority**:
1. Most efficient: Invoke situation Aspects created by you or allies (free if you created them)
2. High impact: Invoke "Graffiti Mage Who Paints with Living Ink" on Crafts rolls (thematic + effective)
3. Emergency: Invoke multiple Aspects on critical rolls (costs add up but guarantees success)

**Compel Management**:
- Accept compels that generate interesting scenes—fate points are worth the complications
- Refuse compels that feel wrong—you can spend a fate point to refuse
- Suggest your own compels to earn GM trust: "Should 'The Swarm Hungers' make this harder?"

**Creating Advantages**:
Use your high skills to create stackable Aspects, then unleash 2-3 free invokes on one massive roll:
- **Crafts +4**: "Protective Ward," "Insect-Infused Paint," "Glyph of Confusion"
- **Lore +3**: "I Know Their Weakness," "Ancient Symbol of Binding"
- **Athletics +3**: "High Ground," "Escape Route Mapped"
- **Notice +2**: "They're Hiding Something," "Secret Cultist Sign"

**Stress Management**:
- **Physical Stress**: Only 2 boxes—AVOID physical conflict
- **Mental Stress**: 3 boxes—slightly better, but still fragile
- **Use Consequences narratively**: "Roaches Feeding Frenzy (Mild)" or "The Moths Marked Me (Moderate)" create story hooks

**Recommended Starting Style**: Try Style 1 (Urban Ritual Mage) or Style 2 (Hit-and-Run Tagger) for your first session—both leverage your highest skills, feel immediately powerful, and are mechanically straightforward. As you get comfortable, blend styles based on the situation.

**Most Important**: Fate Core rewards creative Aspect use. Vesper's Aspects are double-edged by design—the most fun play style is the one that engages your Aspects aggressively, both invoking AND accepting compels. Vesper is built for dramatic tension. Lean into it.
