## Character Summary

Felix is an unassuming, well-dressed man who operates in the city's supernatural underground. He "doesn't do violence," but his voice carries a weight that can break a man's mind. He is the guy who knows everyone, but trusts no one. Felix's power lies not in magic or muscle, but in information, connections, and the ability to make people do what he wants - willingly or otherwise.

## Quick Start: How to Play Felix

### Core Strategy
Felix excels at social manipulation, information gathering, and indirect conflict resolution. He should avoid direct physical confrontation and instead:

1. **Gather Information First:** Use Contacts and Investigate before acting
2. **Position Yourself as Indispensable:** Make others need you more than they want to hurt you
3. **Use Your Voice:** The Voice of Command stunt lets you use your best skill (Rapport +4) to intimidate
4. **Leverage Your Network:** Your contacts are your weapon and your shield
5. **Always Have an Exit:** Plan your escape before entering any dangerous situation

### Recommended Starting Play Style
**The Velvet Glove** - Lead with charm (Rapport), use Cold Reader to discover what people want, then offer it with strings attached. This style leverages Felix's highest skills and creates narrative opportunities.

## Campaign Integration Tips for GMs

### How to Challenge Felix
1. **Isolate Him:** Put him in situations where his network can't help
2. **Compel His Trouble:** Have his contacts demand payment or betray him at critical moments
3. **Force Hard Choices:** Make him choose between multiple network obligations
4. **Physical Threats:** He's vulnerable to direct violence - make him rely on allies
5. **Time Pressure:** Information gathering takes time; rush him

### How to Showcase Felix
1. **Give Him NPCs:** Provide colorful contacts and rivals to interact with
2. **Reward Investigation:** Let him uncover secrets that drive the plot
3. **Create Social Conflicts:** Negotiations, interrogations, and manipulations
4. **Build Network Storylines:** His contacts should have their own agendas
5. **Let The Voice Shine:** Create moments where his calm command is cinematic

## Example Scenes

### Scene 1: The Negotiation
Felix sits across from a vampire lord who wants information. Felix uses Cold Reader (Empathy +3 with +2 bonus) to discover the vampire's Aspect: "Desperate to Find His Sire." Felix then offers to use his network to locate the sire - for a price. The Voice of Command (Rapport +4) makes it clear this isn't really a negotiation.

### Scene 2: The Escape
Cornered in an alley by werewolf enforcers, Felix invokes "Eyes and Ears on Every Corner" to have spotted a manhole cover earlier. He uses The Voice of Command (Rapport instead of Provoke) to calmly order the lead werewolf to stand down, invoking "A Fate Worse Than Violence" to remind them what happened to the last pack that crossed him. While they hesitate, he escapes through the sewers.

### Scene 3: The Information Play
Before meeting a rival fixer, Felix uses Network Saturation to automatically learn that the rival is secretly working with the fae court. During the meeting, Felix drops this knowledge casually in conversation, creating the Aspect "Off-Balance and Exposed" which he can invoke later. The rival now needs Felix's silence more than his death.
