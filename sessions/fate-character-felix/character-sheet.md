# Felix - The Fixer

**Setting:** Modern Urban Fantasy
**Description:** An unassuming, well-dressed man who operates in the city's supernatural underground. Felix "doesn't do violence," but his voice carries a weight that can break a man's mind. He is the guy who knows everyone, but trusts no one.

## Refresh: 3

## Aspects
- **High Concept:** "I Can Be *Very* Persuasive"
- **Trouble:** "Indebted to a Network of Questionable Loyalty"
- **Aspect 3 (Methodology):** "My Network is My Net Worth"
- **Aspect 4 (Asset):** "Eyes and Ears on Every Corner"
- **Aspect 5 (Reputation):** "A Fate Worse Than Violence"

## Skills
- **Great (+4):** Rapport
- **Good (+3):** Contacts, Empathy
- **Fair (+2):** Provoke, Will, Investigate
- **Average (+1):** Deceive, Notice, Resources, Burglary

## Stunts
- **The Voice of Command (Rapport):** Because you are "Very Persuasive," you can use Rapport instead of Provoke to Intimidate or give orders to someone, provided you are speaking to them calmly and they can hear your voice.
- **Network Saturation (Contacts):** Because you have "Eyes and Ears on Every Corner," once per session you can spend a Fate Point to automatically discover a secret about a person or location without having to make a roll.
- **Cold Reader (Empathy):** Because you can see right through people, you get +2 to Empathy when trying to discover a person's Aspects (like their Trouble or hidden motivations) if you have spoken with them for at least a minute.
