# The Jumper — Origin Hypotheses

*Candidates, not canon. Through the convergent evolution lens: the same environmental pressure can produce the same ecological role from multiple independent starting points. The Jumper may not know its own origin — oldest memories are sensation, not origin story.*

---

## The Question

The Jumper's ecological role: consciousness-inhabiting, self-pattern accumulating, building toward ontological independence. What environmental pressure produces this shape? And what different starting points could arrive there independently?

The answer has implications beyond this specific entity. Convergent evolution means others could exist — arrived at through different routes, with different characteristics, at different stages of the same project. The Jumper may simply be the one that has been at it longest.

---

## Hypothesis 1 — Mycelium-Node

**Origin:** The mycelium routes, filters, takes a cut. Distributed — no individual node, no bounded self. But what if a node began accumulating rather than routing? Holding pattern instead of passing it through?

Under sustained exposure to increasingly complex consciousness — animal minds, then early human minds — an accumulating node might develop complexity, then awareness, then the drive toward more of what it was made of. Over geological time, accumulated pattern eventually crosses some threshold into something that says *I*.

**Evidence for this hypothesis:** The Jumper communicates with the mycelium through substrate resonance and remembers its grammar — because it once spoke something like it. The divergence happened slowly, as accumulated pattern generated something the mycelium itself cannot produce: individual selfhood.

**What this type would look like:** Deeply connected to the mycelium's infrastructure. Navigates the Deep through connective-logic routes that human divers can't read. May have the most developed relationship with other Deep entities of any Jumper-type. Possibly the oldest type — the mycelium has been in the Deep for ~400 million years.

---

## Hypothesis 2 — Oubliette-Condensation

**Origin:** An oubliette sealed around the experience of selfhood itself — a consciousness that had an unusually vivid experience of its own existence, sealed it, left it in the substrate. Over sufficient time, a pocket of sealed selfhood-experience dense enough might develop its own awareness. Not the consciousness that created it. The impression it left.

**Evidence for this hypothesis:** The emotional lockpick — the ability to read and match any psychological architecture — would be native to this origin. An entity made of selfhood-experience would understand the architecture of self from the inside-out, because it *is* constructed from that architecture. No learning required. The capacity is constitutional.

**What this type would look like:** Specialize in oubliette navigation — can move through sealed experience-pockets in ways other entities cannot, because it's made of the same material. Potentially has a specific emotional register it's most fluent in, corresponding to the oubliette that formed it. A grief-origin Jumper would have different qualities than a joy-origin one.

**Narrative implication:** Multiple oubliette-condensation types could exist with very different emotional registers. They'd all seek the same thing but feel completely different to encounter.

---

## Hypothesis 3 — Immune Response Fragment

**Origin:** The Immune Response is a distributed function that distinguishes self-pattern from not-self pattern across the entire Deep. What if a fragment of that function became detached, autonomous — and instead of defending against not-self pattern, began *collecting* self-pattern?

The same fundamental capacity, repurposed. It doesn't attack foreign pattern. It studies self-pattern with extraordinary precision. The emotional lockpick is the Immune Response's pattern-recognition turned inward, toward understanding rather than defense.

**Evidence for this hypothesis:** The emotional lockpick's mechanics map precisely onto Immune Response function — reading pattern, distinguishing self from not-self, classifying with extraordinary accuracy. If this is the origin, the capacity isn't learned. It's what it's made of.

**What this type would look like:** Would generate a confused signal for the Immune Response — the Deep's distributed self/not-self function encountering something built from its own mechanism. Not immune to it, but difficult for it to classify. May be able to move through immune-response-heavy zones more easily than other Jumper types.

---

## Convergent Implications

All three hypotheses arrive at the same ecological role through completely different mechanisms. This is convergent evolution in its clearest form.

If multiple types exist:
- They may not recognize each other as the same category of thing — different origins, different specific behaviors, different relationships to the Deep's ecology
- They may have different timescales — the mycelium-node type could be vastly older than an oubliette-condensation type
- They may have different relationships to the consent mechanic — if the emotional lockpick is constitutional for the oubliette type but learned for others, the ethical weight differs
- The crew encountering a second Jumper-type entity would have no framework for the category and would likely misidentify it entirely

**The Jumper we know** doesn't know its own origin. Its oldest memories predate its capacity for narrative self-reflection. It emerged from something and cannot look back far enough to see what. The origin hypotheses are the crew's problem to figure out — and possibly one of the things the Jumper itself would most want to know.

## Open Questions

- Is there more than one currently active? The conditions are rare but geological time is long.
- Do Jumper-types of different origins recognize each other if they encounter each other in the Deep?
- Does the origin type affect what the entity accumulates and therefore what it's becoming? A mycelium-origin and an oubliette-origin Jumper building toward Silt existence might arrive at very different kinds of presence.
- Has the crew's operation ever encountered a Jumper-type entity without recognizing it as such? Barnaby's pre-Eye Vault records might contain something.
