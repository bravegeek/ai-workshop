# Horror Watchlist

Relevant to Ren's character and horror night worldbuilding. Personal watch order.

Already seen: Get Out, Alien, Midsommar.

## Watch in this order

1. **Hereditary** (2018) — Ari Aster. Same director as Midsommar, more architecturally visible, darker.
2. **The Thing** (1982) — John Carpenter. Paranoia, practical effects, horror of not knowing who is still themselves.
3. **The Shining** (1980) — Kubrick. Textbook craft. Understand this and you understand what IO is building toward.
4. **Rosemary's Baby** (1968) — Polanski. Where the paranoia-of-not-being-believed tradition comes from.
5. **The Babadook** (2014) — Jennifer Kent. Grief as monster. Small scale, hits hard.
6. **The Wailing** (2016) — Na Hong-jin. Ren's pick. Watch this last — it needs the vocabulary.

## Bonus when ready to go deeper

- **The Devil's Backbone** (2001) — del Toro. Beautiful ghost story, emotional rather than frightening.
- **Lake Mungo** (2008) — documentary format, grief, quiet devastation. Almost no conventional horror mechanics.
- **Audition** (1999) — Miike. Slow domestic first half, structural trick that lands hard.
