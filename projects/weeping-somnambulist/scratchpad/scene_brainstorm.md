# Scene Brainstorm — Extraction Events, Dream Space, Close Calls

A holding list. Nothing committed to canon yet — ideas to chew on.

---

## Extraction Events

**The wrong person** — IO arrives in an executive's layer and finds material from decades before the target's birth. She's inside an inherited psychic space — a parental legacy running like a subroutine. The thing the client paid for is there, but it belongs to a dead man. She extracts it anyway. She and Ren have a conversation afterward about what that means.

**Already visited** — IO drops into a target's layer and finds fresh tracks. Someone has been there before her, recently. She reads the methodology and doesn't recognize the practitioner. She doesn't find out who until two jobs later.

**The P&L layer** — a corporate target whose dream space is perfectly legible, structured like a quarterly report. Everything profitable, everything attributed. Except one quarter keeps getting restated. The thing IO came for is inside the restatement.

**The speech that never starts** — a politician's layer runs as a perpetual pre-rally. The crowd keeps filling, the stage stays empty. What he's hiding is in the machinery behind the stage — the scaffolding the audience was never meant to see.

## Silly Sisters

**The childhood space** — something they built when they were young and didn't know what they were doing. IO kept trying to make it structurally sound. Nia kept making it stranger. A treehouse that turns sideways. A library where all the books contain color instead of words. They still visit it.

**Nia telegraphs** — Nia's pranks in dream space are affectionate rather than cruel. She always telegraphs slightly so IO knows it's her — a tell so small it's almost not there, a kind of mercy. IO pretends not to see it coming anyway.

**The gift argument** — IO trying to figure out what to give Ren. Nia helping by manifesting options directly. Nia has strong opinions and no patience for IO's overthinking. The argument becomes the environment.

## Of Course Ren

**Ren's layer, first visit** — IO has her possession. Arrives somewhere extremely organized. Everything indexed, cross-referenced, the Dewey Decimal System of the unconscious. IO is delighted. Ren is horrified at what this says about her.

**Nia's archive, not Nia** — after the Blooming, Ren uses Nia's possession. She doesn't find Nia. She finds the Eye's 150-year indexed record of Nia — organized memory rather than presence. She can't tell the difference at first. When she can, she stays anyway.

**Being visited** — Nia comes to Ren in shallow sleep without Ren having sought it. Nia doesn't speak. She sits next to Ren and they both look at something. Ren logs it in the morning: *"N. present, approximately four minutes. No content. Filed."*

**Horror night, Ren's perspective** — what it looks like from inside the velociraptor. The moment IO's methodology becomes visible as a fingerprint. What it feels like when Nia arrives and Ren genuinely doesn't see it.

## Oubliettes

**A space that's still running** — IO drops through an oubliette and finds a fold that belongs to no living mind and isn't TSG. A lost practitioner's layer, still operating on its own. No mind driving it. The genre logic running on residue.

**The Eye's wake** — IO following a ley line current and recognizing she's in the Eye's oubliette trail. The space has the specific quality of reference-beam absence — like a room where a light was on for a long time and then went out.

**Deliberately constructed** — an oubliette someone built on purpose. Not a natural fold. They knew what they were doing. Or thought they did.

**A child's space** — entered the Psychosphere before the architecture of adult cognition was in place. Not dark. Stranger than dark. IO surfaces shaken and can't explain it to Ren in terms Ren's instruments would recognize.

## Drift Dives

**Before the operation** — IO and Nia drifting together in the early period, no target, no extraction. IO trying to maintain orientation. Nia not needing to. They find a confluence node neither knew was there. Budapest visible from deep water as warmth, as direction, as home.

**Solo drift, lost thread** — the possession IO was using for navigation loses its resonance. She's in undifferentiated space with no thread. She finds her way back by reading gradient instead — and realizes this is the first time she's trusted the Mordaunt attunement without a tool in her hand.

**Zone 2** — IO at the edge of where Ren dissolved. Seeing what Ren saw. Having the Resonance Collection between herself and dissolution. The difference is not courage; it's engineering. She holds that quietly after.

**Nia going further** — told retrospectively, through the archive or a recovered thread. Nia drifting past anywhere IO has been, finding something with the character of another Sleeper's gradient. She doesn't recognize it. She brings back a sense-memory IO can't decode — something that isn't Budapest, isn't human, isn't wrong exactly, but is very large.

## Close Calls

**Containment** — the immune response isn't rejection, it's enclosure. IO is inside the cell the target's subconscious built around the material. She surfaces without what she came for. Getting out is the job.

**Target arrives** — REM window shifts mid-visit. The target enters their own space while IO is already inside. She has to become furniture. Not hide — be part of the dream. Still there when they leave.

**The flare** — early period, before the Resonance Collection is complete. IO starts to dissolve at Zone 2 depth. Imposes hard enough to survive. The cost: signal bright enough to attract attention from the Deep. She surfaces fast. Something notices. It doesn't follow — but she knows it clocked her.

**The vacant layer** — IO tracks, navigates, arrives. The space is empty. Not defended — vacant. The target died before she got there. She didn't know until she surfaced and called Ren. She and Ren sit with it for a minute. Then Ren asks if she's billing the client for the night.
