## I. Current Artifact Inventory: Power Classification

### Classification System
Artifacts are classified by **Semantic Density** (depth of origin) and **Operational Class** (functional scope):

- **Class 1-2 (Surface Tools)**: Shallow origin, single function, minimal psychic footprint
- **Class 3 (Thermocline Tools)**: Transition-zone artifacts, bridge logic systems
- **Class 4 (Deep Artifacts)**: High symbolic weight, reality-warping capabilities
- **Class 5 (Abyssal Artifacts)**: Leviathan-class, generate spatial anomalies

## III. Identified Gaps in Operational Capabilities

### 1. **Defensive/Combat Tools**: Zero combat-rated artifacts
- IO's loadout is entirely utility/support
- No mention of weapons for hostile entity encounters
- Kaelen's Glitch-Blade is the only combat tool (personal, not team-shared)

### 2. **Emergency Stabilization**: No contingency for failed extractions
- Seed volatility is a ticking clock with no pause button
- No backup power if Heart of the Storm fails
- No emergency extraction tools if a diver is trapped

### 3. **Reconnaissance/Stealth**: Limited pre-infiltration intel
- Whispering Iron requires physical insertion (high risk)
- No remote surveillance beyond "The Tap" (audio-only buoy)
- No disguise tools for divers (rely on Method Acting alone)

### 4. **Data Storage/Analysis**: No portable knowledge capture
- Ghost Files from the Eye are server-based (not field-accessible)
- No way to record visual evidence of discoveries
- Memory-Jars are "low fidelity" (inadequate for complex data)

### 5. **Crew-Specific Gaps**:
- **Elara**: No tools that amplify her Empath/Manifestation abilities
- **Kaelen**: Glitch-Blade mentioned but no support tech for his Anchor role
- **Ren**: Technomancer has no autonomous assistants (all manual operation)

## IV. Suggested New Artifacts (Creative Muse Proposals)

### **TIER 1: Personal Equipment (Class 2-3)**

#### A. **The Echo-Locket** — Class 2 (Identity Anchor)
- **Origin**: Zone 2 (Shallows), extracted from a "Returned Soldier" memory
- **Form**: Brass locket on a chain, contains a micro-resonator tuned to the wearer's baseline ego-frequency
- **Function**:
  - Passive: Pulses wearer's "identity signature" every 30 seconds
  - Active: Emergency "reality check" if diver is experiencing Narrative Capture
  - Vibrates violently if wearer's sense of self drops below 60% (pre-ego-death warning)
- **Limitation**:
  - Only works if worn continuously (can't be equipped mid-crisis)
  - Drains slowly over 8 hours (requires charging in HQ cistern overnight)
- **Tactical Use**: Prevents forgetting mission objectives in Zone 4+
- **Lore**: Originally a WWI soldier's keepsake; the obsessive focus on "home" crystallized into a protective field
- **Power Level**: Low-Medium (defensive utility)

---

#### B. **Drift-Wax Earplugs** — Class 1 (Sensory Filter)
- **Origin**: Zone 1 (Surface), harvested from "White Noise" static zones
- **Form**: Translucent, waxy plugs that mold to ear canal
- **Function**:
  - Filters out "psychic noise" (anglers, lure-lights, siren calls)
  - Does NOT block Caerulite-Shard comms (different frequency)
  - Allows wearer to hear "true sound" vs. "desire sound"
- **Limitation**:
  - Single-use (melts after 2 hours of exposure to Deep)
  - Does not protect against visual lures
- **Tactical Use**: Counters Angler ambushes, allows focus in chaotic Shallow zones
- **Lore**: Byproduct of the "Null-Glass" synthesis experiments (failed prototype repurposed)
- **Power Level**: Low (consumable)

---

#### C. **The Compass Rose** — Class 3 (Navigation Aid)
- **Origin**: Zone 3 (Thermocline), extracted from a "Lost Cartographer" memory
- **Form**: Tarnished bronze compass, but needle points toward "Narrative Center" instead of North
- **Function**:
  - Always points toward the strongest "Story Gravity" in current zone
  - Warns of Narrative Loops (needle spins rapidly)
  - Can be "keyed" to a specific archetype (e.g., "Betrayal," "Hope") to track thematic patterns
- **Limitation**:
  - Useless in Zone 1 (too many competing narratives)
  - Requires manual recalibration every 30 minutes (twist outer ring)
  - Does not distinguish between "safe" and "dangerous" narrative centers
- **Tactical Use**: Prevents aimless wandering in Zone 4, helps locate artifact origin points
- **Lore**: Belonged to a 19th-century explorer who died mapping "Dream Currents"; his obsession became the tool
- **Power Level**: Medium (utility)

### **TIER 2: Combat/Defense (Class 3-4)**

#### D. **The Orphan's Shroud** — Class 3 (Stealth Cloak)
- **Origin**: Zone 2 (Shallows), crystallized from "Forgotten Child" memories
- **Form**: Tattered grey wool shawl, frayed edges, smells faintly of rain
- **Function**:
  - Passive: Reduces wearer's "narrative weight" by 70% (becomes background NPC)
  - Active: Full invisibility for 30 seconds by embracing "absolute loneliness" emotion
  - NPCs/Immune System treat wearer as "scenery" (not worth investigating)
- **Limitation**:
  - Does NOT work against Deep Parasites (they sense logic fields, not narratives)
  - Requires wearer to suppress all emotional output (exhausting)
  - Breaks immediately if wearer speaks or attacks
- **Tactical Use**: Infiltration, bypassing hostile NPCs, escaping Immune System attention
- **Lore**: Composite of dozens of "invisible child" memories (neglected, unseen); their collective yearning to disappear became armor
- **Power Level**: High (stealth), Fragile (easily broken)

#### E. **The Penitent's Gauntlet** — Class 4 (Symbolic Weapon)
- **Origin**: Zone 4 (Deep), forged from "Guilt Complex" symbolic weight
- **Form**: Iron gauntlet, far heavier than it appears (15kg despite looking like 1kg), engraved with apologies in 40 languages
- **Function**:
  - Converts "emotional mass" into kinetic force
  - Punch damage scales with user's current guilt/regret level

- **Limitation**:
  - Requires wearer to consciously dwell on personal failures (psychologically taxing)

- **Tactical Use**: Combat against entities, moving "unmovable" objects, breaking Narrative Loops by force
- **Lore**: Originally a Zone 4 "Grief Anchor" that a diver tried to punch in rage; the paradox crystallized into a weapon
- **Power Level**: Very High (combat/utility), High Psychological Cost

#### F. **The Smoke-Signal Flare** — Class 2 (Emergency Extraction)
- **Origin**: Zone 2 (Shallows), derived from "SOS" distress dreams
- **Form**: Red glass cylinder, size of a road flare, etched with Morse code
- **Function**:

## VII. Power Creep Mitigation

To prevent artifact bloat:
- **One In, One Out**: For each new artifact added, sell or lose one existing artifact
- **Maintenance Tax**: All Class 4+ artifacts require weekly upkeep (or malfunction)
- **Narrative Cost**: Using high-power artifacts should trigger plot complications
- **Scarcity**: Rare materials (Indigo Fungus, Lethe-Light) should remain limited
