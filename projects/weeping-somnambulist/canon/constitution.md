# Weeping Somnambulist: Constitution

*One page. A compass, not a rulebook. Use it when something feels wrong but you can't say why.*

---

## What This Story Is

A story about people who refined curiosity into exploration into science, operating inside a system so much older and larger than they are that the science keeps failing to contain it. The Deep is not the enemy. It is the environment. The question is what it costs to move through it consciously, and what you become in the process.

---

## The Questions It Is Always Asking

- **What does it cost to know something?** The Architects died of Lethe-Light. Ren can't dive. Nia dissolved. Knowledge in this world has a price denominated in self.

- **What do you owe a world you're passing through?** The crew takes from the Deep — artifacts, Caerulite, experience. The Deep is not asking for anything back. That doesn't mean nothing is owed.

- **What is the relationship between accumulation and identity?** The Jumper is writing itself into existence through collected lives. TSG accumulated over geological time into something that wants to dissolve boundary. The Vault grows a new room for every artifact brought in. The Cistern replays what it absorbed. Everything in this world is shaped by what it has held.

- **What does consent mean when the terms weren't fully disclosed?** Lorcan's ink was given before he could choose. The Jumper engineers the conditions for yes. The crew operates in a building whose original purpose they're still discovering. Nobody here signed up for exactly this.

- **How do you hold yourself together when something vast is pressing against you?** This is Ren's question most directly. It is everyone's question eventually.

---

## Tonal Principles

**The uncanny comes from scale and indifference, not from evil.** The Deep doesn't want to hurt anyone. It responds the way an ecosystem responds — not to what you mean, but to what you are. The friction is the friction of being the wrong shape for the environment. Horror here is ecological.

**Everything has a mechanism.** The world works through precise physical, ecological, and psychic logic. Nothing is vague. When something seems magical it has a mechanism that science could eventually reach. The Architects started reaching it. The crew is continuing that work.

**The mundane and the vast coexist without comment.** The crew has disagreements about equipment and eats meals and gets tired. They do this while operating inside something older than language. Neither fact cancels the other.

**Characters earn their contradictions.** Nobody here has flaws in the dramatic sense. They have situations — obligations that pull against each other, knowledge that changes what they can want, relationships with things that don't share their timescale. The tension is structural, not moral.

**Specificity over abstraction.** A grief oubliette has different substrate than a horror oubliette. The Indigo Ink is a declaration, not a spell. The Lethe-Light bleaches Caerulite-dysregulated memory. Name the mechanism. Show the work.

---

## What This Story Is Not

- **Not heroes vs. monster.** There is no monster. There are forces, pressures, and consequences.
- **Not the Deep as enemy.** It is indifferent. Indifference is harder than hostility.
- **Not knowledge as simply good or bad.** It is expensive. The price varies.
- **Not horror through malice.** If something in a scene is malicious in the conventional sense, ask whether that's the right register.
- **Not resolution through power.** The problems here are not solved by being stronger or smarter than the thing you're facing.

---

## The Test

When something feels wrong but you can't say why, ask:

1. Does this treat the Deep as an enemy rather than an environment?
2. Does this resolve through power rather than cost?
3. Does this introduce malice where indifference would be harder and truer?
4. Does this flatten a character's contradiction into a flaw to be overcome?
5. Does this work by abstraction where a mechanism would be more honest?

If yes to any of these, the scene is drifting. Pull it back toward the questions.
