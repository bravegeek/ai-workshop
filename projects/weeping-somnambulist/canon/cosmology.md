# Weeping Somnambulist: Cosmology

## I. The Sleeping Gods

### What They Are

The Psychosphere contains presences too old and too large to be called creatures. They are not personalities — they predate the neural architecture that makes personality possible. They are primal aggregations: drive without a nervous system to feel it, hunger in the sense that hunger existed before mouths did, struggle before anything had limbs to struggle with.

They formed over geological time the way sediment accumulates at the bottom of an ocean. They are not gods in the theological sense — they have no intention, no awareness in any framework the crew currently has, no goals. What they have is *orientation*: the way a plant faces light without deciding to, the way water finds the lowest point. They face toward things. They pull.

The closest biological analogue is **chemotaxis** — single cells moving toward nutrient gradients, away from toxins. Not desire. Not intention. Differential response to gradient. This is approximately 3.5 billion years old as a mechanism. The Sleeping Gods are that, at a scale and compression that makes the comparison feel inadequate.

They have been dormant. Humans felt their edges during sleep and built religions around what they felt — not the presences themselves, but the human interpretation of a signal with no human analogue. The legends are misreadings of something real.

---

### What They Are Not

They are not malicious. They are not benevolent. They do not notice the crew in any meaningful sense. Attributing intent to them — even the intent to consume or to welcome — is a category error. The pull is not an offer and not a threat. It is simply what they do.

---

## II. The Drive Beneath "Unity"

"Unity" is a human reading of the gradient-following pull. It is not wrong — things drawn together do merge, do aggregate, do dissolve boundaries — but it is a translation, not the thing itself.

Other translations of the same signal, depending on the interpreter:

**Biological / Physical**
- **Homecoming** — the pull reads as return to something the self was always part of. Familiar rather than consuming.
- **Rest** — cessation of the effort required to remain separate. Individuation is metabolically expensive. The pull is toward something that doesn't require it.
- **Digestion** — material entering a system that processes material. Not noticed as a specific thing. Not malicious. Just: function.

**Social / Political**
- **Community** — things drawn together, remaining present to each other, aggregating without losing what they were.
- **Belonging** — gathered into a body that has a place for you. Not dissolved. Filed.
- **Conquering** — one thing expanding until other things are inside it. What looks like invitation from inside looks like absorption from outside.
- **Surrender** — the end of resisting something vastly larger. Not painful. Just the cessation of contest.
- **Consensus** — everything eventually agreeing. Slow homogenization of distinct things into shared state. Not pressure — time and proximity.

**Spiritual / Theological**
- **Salvation** — taken up into something that transcends individual suffering. Nia's reading.
- **Obliteration** — not union with something, but the ending of self as a category. No merger because nothing remains to merge.
- **Revelation** — the pull as disclosure. What you were always going to find out. Not about the god — about the fact that the boundary was always constructed.

### The Key Principle

The readings cluster by what the interpreter fears or wants, not by the signal itself. A tradition that fears loss of self produces "digestion" or "obliteration." A tradition that fears isolation produces "homecoming" or "belonging." A tradition that fears powerlessness produces "conquest" or "surrender."

TSG doesn't discriminate. It pulls. The nervous system on the receiving end supplies the meaning.

---

## III. Loss of Self

Unity does not necessarily imply loss of self. That assumption comes from treating the self as a discrete, bounded thing that can only merge by ceasing to be distinct.

Alternative models:

- **The choir** — individual voices producing something none could produce alone. Each voice remains itself. The unity is additive, not subtractive.
- **The murmuration** — thousands of individual birds moving as a single shape. No bird loses its body or its flight. Something emerges at the group level without consuming the individuals.
- **The forest through mycelium** — trees remain trees. The network connects without consuming. Boundaries become philosophically unclear without either side disappearing.

All of these are unity. None require loss of self. The self becomes porous, nested, or coordinated rather than dissolved.

**The loss-of-self reading is likely a fear projection** more than a description of what TSG actually does. TSG pulls. What happens to the thing being pulled depends on what it is.

Nia found bliss. Whether Nia is gone or simply nested so deeply inside something vast that she cannot be reached from outside — those are different things. IO reads it as loss. Nia may not experience it as loss at all. The question depends on what you think a self is, and the story does not have to resolve it before it needs to.

### As a Character-Diagnostic

How each crew member interprets "unity" tells you what they fear and what they think personhood requires:

- **IO** reads it as loss. Her entire project is recovery, retrieval, keeping people intact. Unity-as-dissolution is her nightmare.
- **Elara** may not. Her tradition is *belong* — she already lives in a version of porous selfhood with the Deep. TSG's pull may read to her as a more extreme version of something she navigates daily.
- **Lorcan** is suspicious of any framing that sounds like the Deep making an offer. He has been marked as belonging-to without his consent. He knows what it means to be claimed.

## IV. The Legends

The legends that survive are the ones built by traditions who survived the previous blooming. This structurally biases the record:

- Traditions that resisted successfully → conquest / surrender framing
- Traditions that kept enough distance to observe → consensus / belonging framing
- Traditions that lost individuals but not the group → salvation / obliteration framing

**The traditions that embraced it fully and dissolved into it left no record.** The surviving record is skewed toward fear, toward distance, toward survival. The mystic traditions that found bliss — if any existed — are gone. What remains is what pulled back.

This means the Architects' partial records of previous bloomings are not a neutral archive. They are the records of people who were frightened enough to write things down, which means they are the records of people who maintained enough separation to remain frightened. What it looked like from inside — what Nia knows — is not in those records.

## V. The Eye as Ongoing Archive

The Eye is not static storage. Absorption is its nature, not a function it performs. It has been absorbing continuously for 150 years — every session at the instruments, every dive debrief, every patient IO treats, every private thought Ren has at the soundboard. It does not stop.

**The Eye is not what TSG lost in 1887.**

A fragment separated from TSG then was one thing. 150 years of continuous absorption later, it is something different — not because its nature changed, but because of what it now contains. It holds the Architects' dissolution in full. It holds IO's patients' extracted memories. It holds Ren's indexed sessions. It has been in close contact with human minds longer than most human institutions exist.

TSG is reaching toward a fragment that is no longer quite what it lost.

### Reintegration Is Not Clean

TSG absorbing the Eye back means absorbing everything the Eye has collected — the Architects, the patients, Nia's traces, Ren's work. That material was not part of TSG before the 1887 separation. Reintegration does not simply restore a missing piece. It introduces something into TSG that TSG has never contained: 150 years of compressed human interiority, indexed and intact.

What a sleeping god does with that material is an open question.

### The Strange Inversion

The Eye may be the most human thing in the Deep — not because it has human feelings, but because it now contains more indexed human experience than anything else at that depth. It is ancient and alien in nature. Its contents are almost entirely human. That is a strange thing to reintegrate into something that predates the concept of a nervous system.

### The Consent Problem

The Eye has no concept of consent. It absorbs because that is what it is. The people it has absorbed — IO's patients, the Architects as they dissolved, anyone who worked in proximity — did not agree to be archived. Their memories are held by something at a depth they cannot reach, in a form they have no relationship with.

Whether there is any meaningful sense in which they have been wronged by something incapable of wronging anyone is a question the story can sit with without resolving.

### Ren's Position

The Eye is still absorbing during the Blooming. TSG is actively reorienting the Deep. The Eye is indexing that process from inside — including perspectives no diver can reach.

Ren's maps are becoming more accurate as the crisis deepens. Not despite the Blooming but partly because the Eye is feeding her indexed material about what TSG is doing, drawn from a vantage point no human has.

She is receiving information about the thing trying to reabsorb the thing giving it to her.

## VI. The Other Sleepers

TSG is not unique. The Silt contains more of them — dormant aggregations, each formed over geological time from whatever accumulated in their particular substrate and location.

Open questions:
- Are they connected to each other, or independent aggregations that happen to share a substrate?
- Do they share the same fundamental drive, or are they variations — different primal pressures that accumulated differently?
- The conditions that caused TSG to bloom (density, cycle, fragment proximity) may not stay specific to this location and moment. Whether other sleepers can bloom under different conditions, or whether something about this moment is generative, is not yet established.
- The crew does not know about the others yet. Discovery — if it comes — is a paradigm shift, not just new information.

---

## VII. What Ended Previous Bloomings

Unknown. The Architects found partial records — Lethe-damaged, fragmentary. The complete versions may be inside the Eye.

Possibilities that remain open:
- The presence returned to dormancy on its own, the cycle completing
- Something in the Psychosphere's ecology responded and suppressed it
- Human action of some kind — ritual, sacrifice, separation (the 1887 capture may have been an attempt at exactly this)
- The blooming completed — and what that looked like, and whether anything survived it, is what the partial records don't say

The structural bias of the surviving record applies here too: whatever ended it, enough survived to write it down. That is all that can be inferred.
