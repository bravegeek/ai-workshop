# Weeping Somnambulist: Oubliettes

## I. What They Are

An oubliette is a **sealed pocket of preserved experience** — a section of the Psychosphere's normal flow that was interrupted before processing could complete and buried under new material before it could dissipate.

The result is a hard-shelled capsule containing experience frozen at the moment of sealing: the interior preserved exactly as it was, cut off from the Psychosphere's continuous flow, untouched by the time that has passed outside it.

The name comes from the French *oublier* — to forget. These are the things the Psychosphere failed to process and instead walled off.

---

## II. How They Form

Normal Psychosphere processing is continuous: experience flows in, consolidates, sinks, compresses, eventually becomes Silt. An oubliette requires two conditions disrupting this flow:

1. **A sudden overwhelming event** — too much experience generated too fast for normal processing to handle
2. **Rapid burial** — new material flows over the unprocessed spike before it can disperse, sealing it

### Formation Triggers

**Mass death events.** The K-Pg extinction — the asteroid impact that killed the non-avian dinosaurs — generated an unimaginable simultaneous spike across millions of creatures. Too much to process. Overflow sealed into pockets before it could dissipate. The largest known oubliettes likely formed at mass extinction boundaries.

**Civilizational collapse.** A culture that disappeared suddenly rather than slowly — Bronze Age collapse, pre-contact civilizations destroyed by disease in years rather than centuries — generates a spike the Psychosphere cannot process at normal throughput.

**Intense repetition.** A single mind or small group experiencing the same moment with extreme intensity over a long period. Severe recurring trauma can carve a micro-oubliette — the experience becomes too dense to flow. These are the shallowest and most dangerous for casual dreamers.

**Ley line disruption.** A major shift in the Psychosphere's flow that buries a section of active experience under new material before it can disperse — the psychic equivalent of a landslide.

### The Shell

The boundary that forms between sealed experience and the surrounding flow hardens into a **psychic crust** — dense, pressurized, and resistant to the normal decay processes of the Deep. The shell's thickness and integrity depends on how quickly burial occurred and how much material compressed over it afterward. Old shells are thick and hard. Recently formed shells may still be thin and unstable.

---

## III. Depth and Layer

Oubliettes settle at the depth corresponding to **their age and the nature of their contents**. A Cretaceous oubliette compressed and sank over 66 million years to its natural depth in Zone 4–5. A Bronze Age human oubliette sits in Zone 2–3. A micro-oubliette from recent recurring trauma may be in the Shallows.

### Oubliettes Out of Layer

Finding an oubliette at the wrong depth is always significant. It was either moved, dredged, or frozen in place.

**Ley line confluence dredging.** A powerful ley line intersection creates a vortex that pulls Nodes up from the Silt. An ancient oubliette can be surfaced into accessible layers by a confluence event — briefly. It will sink back. This is a **ticking-clock structure**: a pocket from deep time has been dredged into Zone 2. The crew has a window before it descends again.

**Blooming gravity.** As TSG stirs and the Deep reorganizes around it, oubliettes near the gravity well's influence can be held at wrong depths — suspended by the same pull that disrupts Ley line tides. An oubliette that should have sunk back into the Silt remains accessible because something far below is holding the currents still. This is not a gift. It means whatever is in that pocket is now sitting in a layer whose culture has no relationship to it.

**Architect relocation.** The Victorian Architects experimented with relocating oubliettes using the Eye's time-distortion and reality-anchoring properties. The crew may find ancient pockets at wrong depths with no obvious explanation — until they read the Victorian records more carefully.

**Shell degradation.** A very old oubliette's shell thins over time. The contents don't move, but they bleed flavor into adjacent layers — alien sensation, wrong-scale experience, ancient texture leaking through a cracked crust. A zone that feels ancient and profoundly wrong without a visible oubliette may mean you're standing next to one losing its seal.

## IV. The Non-Human Depth Problem

Most oubliettes in the accessible layers (Zone 1–3) are human in origin. The further down you go, the older and less human the contents become.

The current maps — inherited from the Architects — chart only what human divers have successfully navigated. The non-human layers are unmapped. This is not because they don't exist. It is because no one has yet gone deep enough and come back coherent enough to describe them.

### The Blend

Every dreamer already experiences the blend of all life's contributions in ordinary sleep. The non-human texture in surface dreams — the strange non-linearity, the animal instinct that surfaces, the sense of scale belonging to no human life — is the Psychosphere's mixed nature showing through at low concentration. Depth shifts the ratio. Human narrative thins. Older material becomes the signal.

Concentration also varies horizontally. Sleeping above old-growth forest versus sleeping above a city changes the local character of what a diver enters. A wilderness entry point makes non-human layers more present and more accessible than a concrete basement.

### Entry into Non-Human Oubliettes

The **Method Actor mechanic breaks down** in proportion to how non-human the oubliette's origin is. Method Acting works because it gives the diver a role to perform — a narrative to slot into. Non-human oubliettes have no roles. No narrative structure. The Immune System's response isn't attack; it's incomprehension. Which may be worse.

| Origin | Method Actor Viability | Risk Profile |
|--------|----------------------|--------------|
| Human (recent) | Full | Standard |
| Human (ancient) | Partial — pre-verbal, pre-narrative | Ego dissolution |
| Early mammal | Minimal — emotional only, no story | Identity erosion |
| Reptile / dinosaur | None | Sensory overload, madness |
| Insect | None — requires hive dissolution | Loss of individual self |
| Plant / fungal | None — wrong timescale entirely | Temporal dissociation |

### Ama's Clan Training

Ama has training from a tradition that deliberately sought high concentrations of non-human life — extended time in deep wilderness, fungal networks, old-growth environments — to develop tolerance for alien experience.

The skill is not immunity. It is **patience and non-interpretation**: the ability to sit inside an alien experience without the mind reaching for a human narrative framework that doesn't exist there. Most divers confronting a dinosaur oubliette would break when that reach finds nothing. Ama has been trained to stop reaching.

This makes her the only crew member currently capable of navigating a dinosaur oubliette without serious psychological risk — and even for her it would be at the edge of what's survivable.

## V. What You Find — The Rewards

Oubliettes are the highest-risk, highest-reward targets in the Deep.

### Extinct Biology
A Cretaceous oubliette contains sensory experience of organisms that no longer exist. Their biochemistry, their ecosystems, their relationships to a world radically different from the present. Applied: medicinal compounds from extinct plants. Genetic material with no modern equivalent. The experience of ecosystems the surface world has no record of.

### Lost Human Knowledge
An oubliette from a destroyed civilization may hold the only existing record of things irretrievably gone: a dead language spoken fluently, technologies never recorded elsewhere, mathematical or philosophical frameworks that didn't survive. This is the highest-value salvage the crew can recover — and it cannot be extracted by any other means.

### Compressed Artifacts
Applying the Diamond Theory: an oubliette is a pressure vessel. Whatever crystallizes inside has been under compression for the entire period since formation. A Cretaceous artifact carries 66 million years of psychic pressure behind it. The density and power of such an object is nearly incomprehensible by current artifact standards.

### Lost Emotional States
Ways of experiencing the world that the modern mind can no longer generate organically — pre-industrial relationship with nature, pre-monetary relationship with time, extinct forms of community. There is a market for these among those who know what to ask for.

### Historical Intelligence
The Psychosphere has no written records. It has raw experience. An oubliette from a moment of civilizational collapse contains what actually happened — not the history that survived, but the experience of those who didn't. The intelligence value of this is significant and largely unexplored.

## VI. Casual Dreamers and Accidental Entry

**Deep oubliettes** — inaccessible to casual dreamers. The depth barrier provides natural protection.

**Shallow oubliettes** — possible. A trauma oubliette near the Shallows can catch a dreamer whose emotional frequency resonates with its content. The entry condition for many shallow oubliettes is emotional resonance, not skill. Someone with recurring severe trauma may accidentally graze a matching oubliette.

**Proximity without entry.** The more common casual experience: not entering an oubliette but pressing against its shell. Certain psychiatric phenomena — treatment-resistant recurring nightmares, night terrors, the specific horror of sleep paralysis — may be accidental contact with shallow oubliette shells. The dreamer experiences the bleed from outside without breaking through.

**Geographic resonance.** Sleeping in a location with traumatic history places the dreamer physically closer to relevant pockets. A hotel built on a massacre site, a house where someone suffered over years — the resonance between physical location and Psychosphere topology can create proximity the dreamer did not intend.

**Implication for the crew:** people with chronic sleep disorders or recurring trauma nightmares may be accidentally grazing oubliettes. Occasionally, one gets stuck — not fully inside, not fully outside. The crew may be hired to extract them.

## VII. Open Questions

- What is the smallest possible oubliette? Can a single person's recurring trauma create one, or does it require a minimum number of minds?
- Are there oubliettes from before complex animal life — purely microbial or chemical experience? What would be inside, and could any mind survive contact?
- Can an oubliette be deliberately created? If so, by whom and for what purpose?
- What happens to the shell when an oubliette is entered and exited? Does it reseal, degrade, or collapse?
- The Architects almost certainly attempted to cultivate or relocate oubliettes. What did they find, and did it contribute to their dissolution?
