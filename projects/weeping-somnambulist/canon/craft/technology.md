### 1. The Caerulite-Shards (The Comms)

*   **Properties:**

*   **Application:**

## II. Caerulite — Harvest & Forms

### The Indigo Fungus in the Psychosphere
A bioluminescent deep-dream organism. In the Deep it appears as cold blue mycelial threads running through the Silt — sparse and easily missed at shallow depths, dense and almost architectural at Zone 4+, clustering along ley lines and around oubliettes where concentrated memory makes good substrate. Fruiting bodies resemble deep-sea organisms: slow-pulsing, cold light. The signature is more felt than seen — a slight cold quality, a blue resonance, something that reads as threshold. Trained divers recognise it. Untrained ones walk through it.

### The Extraction Mechanic
The diver is the carrier. Caerulite encodes into the diver during the dive — the compound embeds in thinned myelin, the pattern held in the default mode network. Upon surfacing, the diver places their hands in attuned water and enters a coherent state: not effort, but attunement. Heart-brain coherence — the diver's electromagnetic field organises, and the Caerulite in them moves toward what resonates. A glow appears in the water, bioluminescent blue. The Caerulite precipitates out.

**The fading window:** Dream memory fades on waking as noradrenaline floods back and suppresses hippocampal consolidation. The pattern in the diver's mind degrades with it. What holds the pattern: sensory specificity about the harvest site (the exact cold of the Silt, the texture of where the fungus clustered), the emotional charge of the location, an inner tone held during surfacing, and accumulated practice. First dive to a new site produces a poor harvest regardless of abundance. The best harvest sites are the most memorable ones — emotionally charged locations near oubliettes or ley lines produce Caerulite that is both higher quality and easier to retain.

**Biophoton emission:** The glow is not incidental. Thinned divers emit elevated biophoton radiation — measurable light from heightened neural activity — radiating through the hands into the water. A heavily thinned diver glows faintly in darkness regardless of whether they're extracting. Lorcan notices this about IO before she acknowledges it herself.

### Product Hierarchy
Different traditions refined the raw compound to different degrees:
- **Glow (the tea):** Caerulite-infused water, drunk directly. Variable dose, gentler onset, shorter duration. The baseline product across most traditions. What IO distributes.
- **Crystals:** The water evaporated or distilled. Caerulite precipitates as tiny blue-white crystals, faintly luminous. Concentrated, tradeable, longer shelf life. Some cultures discovered this independently.
- **Smoked crystals:** Crystals burned and inhaled. Fast onset, intense, brief — ceremonial use. A shaman who needs to go under quickly without full preparation.
- **Caerulite-Shards:** Crystals refined further by the Architect process into stable, precisely dosed form. The pharmaceutical end of the spectrum. IO's communications technology depends on this level of precision. Post-reintegration the crew has their Shard stockpile but cannot produce more.

### How Different Traditions Harvested
The water mechanic was discovered independently across cultures; the Architects were not the first.
- **Sacred site cultivation:** Recognise where the fungus grows densely — ley confluences, thermal springs, thin-barrier ground — and go to it. Manage the site as sacred. The fungus is not removed; the practitioner comes to it repeatedly, harvesting tea from the site water directly.
- **Dream incubation:** Sleep in the sacred site over multiple nights, absorbing spores passively through the air. The Greek Asclepian temples and Egyptian dream sanctuaries operated this way without fully understanding the mechanism. Priests managed a growing site; incubants were slowly dosed.
- **Communal brew and ceremony:** Harvested material steeped and consumed collectively. Creates a shared dreamspace — wider but shallower than individual access, different phenomena than solo diving.
- **Topical and ink:** Caerulite worked into preparations rubbed into skin or used in ritual tattooing. Sustained low-level permeability rather than a dive. Lorcan's tradition. The ink-making is itself an extraction — a warden after a deep boundary working, hands in prepared water, drawing out the concentrate that becomes the ink base.
- **Smoke:** Dried fungal material burned in ritual context. Oldest and crudest method; widely distributed.

**What the Architects contributed:** Not the discovery that water works, but the engineering of controlled conditions — the Cistern as sacred spring at scale, the heptagonal geometry shaping the acoustic field (cymatics), the Eye stabilising the development process, and the refinement process that produced stable Shards from raw crystal. They found shamanic crystals, recognised what they were, and built a laboratory around the phenomenon.

## III. The HQ Tech (The Cistern)

### 1. The Witness (Artifact Retrieval)

**Crew term:** *Witness.* **Architect term:** *Reliquary.* The object predates both names — every tradition that developed artifact extraction arrived at the same requirement independently.

**What it is:** A personal object of significance to the diver. Not a specialist tool manufactured for the purpose — something already meaningful. A pocket watch, a photograph, a piece of jewellery, a worn coin. The Architects favoured small brass instruments: compass housings, empty locket cases, the mechanical movements of clocks stripped of their faces. The crew uses whatever they already carry.

**Why significance matters:** The diver is the carrier — the artifact pattern encodes into them during the dive and must be held precisely during the ascent and surfacing. The Witness receives that impression. A random object holds the pattern loosely; the pattern bleeds during transit and the development produces something degraded or incomplete. A personally significant object holds tight. The diver's emotional relationship to the object is the binding agent. There is no substitute for this. The Architects spent years trying to engineer around it and eventually stopped trying.

**What comes back:** The artifact develops in the storm-charged pool. The Witness surfaces with it — but changed. The development process leaves residue in the object. A shallow, simple artifact barely touches it. Something old and heavy from Zone 4+ leaves a mark. Use the same Witness repeatedly and it accumulates: each carry overlays the object's personal meaning with what it has held. A veteran diver's Witness is legible as a record of their career — if you knew how to read it. Eventually the personal meaning is so overlaid with carried history that the object is no longer quite theirs. Most divers retire a Witness before that point. Some don't.

**What happens if significance fails mid-carry:** The pattern slips. Not catastrophically — but the development produces something partial, or something adjacent to what was intended. Divers do not attempt deep carries with objects they are ambivalent about.

**The development process (Storm-Forging — historical):** The Witness was submerged in the storm-charged Cistern pool. The storm energy activated the water to maximum attuning; the pattern transferred from the Witness into physical reality. The Eye's Lethe-Light then acted as stop bath — halting development precisely and clearing psychosphere residue from the water. Without the stop bath, development continues: the artifact absorbs storm energy, pool residue, and whatever the water has accumulated, producing something larger and less controlled than intended. Storm-Forging required the Eye as stop bath. With reintegration, the stop bath is gone. The process is now historical — and attempting it produces unpredictable results. See `stories/arc2.md` — the accidental development scene.

**Names across traditions:**
- *Reliquary* — the Architect term. Institutional, sacred-object register. A container for something precious.
- *Witness* — the crew's term. What the object actually does: it observes and accumulates. Possibly a translation of the warden term — Lorcan's tradition named it first.
- *Finné* — the warden/Irish term (*witness*, *one who testifies*). Predates the Architect practice. The wardens used the same object not for extraction but for boundary-working: carrying evidence of what they'd encountered back across the threshold.
- Ama's tradition has no formal term. Her relationship to the Deep is not extractive — she uses an object but resists the frame of tool. When pressed she calls it *the thing I carry*, which is not evasion. It is her actual position.
- Shamanic traditions broadly name it after relationship rather than function: *keeper*, *companion*, *bearer*. The object accompanies rather than captures.

### 2. The Cistern Pool (The Transitional Medium)
*   **Role:** The Cistern water is the transitional medium between the Psychosphere and physical reality — not a metaphor. Caerulite (and other Psychosphere material) can only cross into the physical world through water that is attuned to the Psychosphere. The Architects chose this site because geothermal water already rises through the ley line confluence beneath it. They engineered what was already happening.
*   **After a Ley Line Storm:** The pool takes the full force of the surge. The water moves without being touched, develops slow cymatic patterns shaped by the heptagonal geometry. The psychic cold inverts — warmth that isn't temperature. The accumulated Caerulite activates: the pool glows deep indigo, shifting, brighter where the ley current runs strongest, pulsing with the surge. After the storm passes, the water settles denser and more saturated. An indigo film sits on the surface, dissipating slowly. Each storm leaves a faint stain ring on the pool walls at the high-water mark. Storm water is Glow at an uncontrolled, massive concentration — dangerous to use directly, but the most potent extraction medium available post-reintegration.
*   **Residue:** Each extraction cycle leaves psychic sediment. If unpurged by a Tidal Flush, accumulated fragments affect future extractions.
*   **Medical use:** Tuning forks struck against the pool walls treat psychic decompression after deep dives — the heptagonal acoustics shatter dream-logic residue clinging to a diver's mind.

### 3. The Heart of the Storm (The Eye)
*   **The Reality:** Not a Leviathan. A fragment of one of the ancient presences in the Silt — separated by the Architects in 1887 and held at 80m in rusted anchor chains. The crew treats it as a power source and reality anchor. This is accurate as far as it goes. See `canon/the_eye.md`.
*   **Function:** Generates a Class-5 logic field that stabilizes the HQ, enables depth access, and powers Storm-Forging.
*   **Control:** **Lead eyelids** are mechanically lowered to muffle its gaze. The winch systems that would raise it are shattered. It is not going anywhere.
*   **The Sight (Two States):**
    *   **Passive (The Oracle):** The Eye exists outside linear time. It leaks "Ghost Files" onto the HQ instruments — corrupted records of dives that haven't happened yet, blueprints for technology not yet invented. Unreliable, difficult to interpret, but no one else has precognitive data at all.
    *   **Active (The Gaze):** Retracting the lead eyelids releases **Lethe-Light** into the Cistern. It bleaches whatever is most active in the minds of anyone exposed. Used surgically for the Eye Protocol (Caerulite treatment). Unprotected exposure causes progressive memory loss — the mechanism that killed the Architects.

### 4. IO's Resonance Collection

IO's operative toolkit for the Deep. Not a single device — a curated set of instruments, some built by her, some inherited from the Architects, each tuned to reduce impedance mismatch between her signal and the Deep's substrate at a specific depth or zone character.

**The underlying mechanic — impedance matching and resonance:**
The Deep at each depth has a characteristic resonant profile shaped by what accumulated there over geological time. Narrative-dense shallow zones have one character; pre-linguistic mid-depth another; pre-animal Silt something else entirely. IO's signal is wrong for all of it — she generates friction by being precisely herself in an environment shaped by everything that isn't her. The tools don't change what she is. They add harmonics that reduce destructive interference, making her signal *less wrong* in a specific, controllable way. The ecosystem's response becomes predictable rather than undifferentiated.

Using the wrong tool for the depth doesn't just fail — it can worsen the interference, add dissonance, make her more visible to the immune response rather than less. Tool selection is consequential.

**Pre-dive selection:**
Ren reads the Hum and provides a substrate profile — zone character, dominant frequency, what's active nearby. IO selects against it: what's down there, what frequency is dominant, what she used in similar conditions previously, what it cost. The selection draws on instrument knowledge, accumulated experience, and pattern recognition built through years of reading the Deep through the monocle. She is already most of the way through the dive before she enters the water.

**Failure mode:**
She reads conditions correctly, selects appropriately, goes in — and the substrate shifts. Blooming reorganization, or she's descended past what the pre-dive profile covered. Now she's mid-depth with tools calibrated for something that no longer matches. She cannot surface to reselect. She works with what she has. Improvisation is what she's worst at. This is the specific vulnerability her preparation is designed to avoid and cannot always prevent.

**The Architect instruments:**
The Vault contains tools the Architects left — some finished, some mid-development when the dissolution took the maker. Some are tuned to frequencies the crew has never navigated: depths the Architects reached, things they found and never documented. IO can read the calibration without fully understanding what it was designed for. She brings instruments on instinct. They do unexpected things.

Some of these respond better to her than to anyone else on the crew — not because of standing, but because her resonant profile is descended from the people who made them. The calibration was built around a Mordaunt nervous system. She picks up a tool that has been in the Vault for 150 years and it fits in a way that is slightly, unnervingly, as if it was made for her. Because it was.

**What it isn't:**
Not credentials. Not standing. Not a claim the Deep evaluates and accepts. The ecosystem doesn't recognize her as belonging — it encounters a specific, legible signal and responds to that rather than to the general noise of a foreign organism. She is always a foreign organism. The tools make her a *known* one, briefly and locally.

### 5. Chronometry
*   **Mechanical Chronometers:**
    *   **Necessity:** Electronic clocks fail near the Eye. Time Dilation (1 min = 1 hour) makes internal time-sensing impossible.
    *   **Design:** Over-sized, brass, wind-up clocks worn on the forearm.
    *   **The Rule:** "Trust the Tick." If the hand moves, time has passed, regardless of how you feel.

### 5. The Pale Lens (Threshold Detector)
*   **Class:** Class-4 Artifact.
*   **Origin:** Recovered by the Architects from a Dead Drift — a coastal city that lost its relationship to language and, eventually, to itself. The Architects used it as a navigational aid: the Lens was designed to guide things home, and they used it to orient divers returning from deep excursions. When IO found the Institute and inherited its contents, she recognised what the Lens could become and recalibrated it for the inverse function — not guiding home but identifying what doesn't belong. The Architects used it to find their way in. IO uses it to control what gets through. Same tool, opposite orientation.
*   **Location:** The Heptagonal Antechamber. Mounted on a fixed brass pedestal, sweeping continuously.
*   **Function:** Projects Null-Light — light that casts no shadows. Reads *fit*: whether something has the right relationship to this space and the people in it. Not lie detection. An intruder's cover story doesn't register as a lie — it registers as wrong, the way a forgery looks wrong under the right light. An entity from the Deep with no surface anchor registers as mostly Psychosphere with a thin surface layer.
    *   Reads in both directions — unauthorized entry and unauthorized egress.
    *   When it flags, it triggers the Mechanical Lock — a heavy brass mechanism controlling the elevator shaft from the Antechamber level. The elevator does not move. Whatever was flagged is now in a heptagonal space with collapsing-obsession acoustics and no exit. The lock is not the security system. The room is.
*   **Post-reintegration status:** The Lens should have gone dark when the Eye reintegrated — IO's analysis gave no scenario where it survived the loss of the logic field. It kept working. Her fear was legitimate; the survival is not reassuring. After 150 years of continuous operation in proximity to a TSG fragment, the Lens absorbed something specific: the Eye's accumulated sense of what belongs in this space, indexed over generations. That imprint — residue of a TSG fragment rather than generic psychic charge — is what the Lens now reads against. Whether this constitutes a faint active connection to TSG whole, or a finite charge that will eventually dissipate, is unknown. IO checks it obsessively. It reads differently than it used to — calibrated to something larger and less organized than the Eye's field. The crew is relearning what its flags mean.
*   **Second arc — open question:** Ren's connection to TSG whole and the Lens's TSG residue may resonate. She may be able to read the Lens more accurately than anyone else — or interface with it in ways that extend its operational life. This matters because IO is not available: she built the system, she understands what the Lens's flags mean, she's the one who checks it obsessively. In the second arc she's stepped back. The crew is running a piece of security infrastructure that shouldn't be working, that they don't fully understand, without the person who built it. If something gets flagged — or something that should be flagged isn't — the person who would know the difference is sitting somewhere in the Institute not leading. IO eventually stops checking. That's not incidental to her arc. It's part of it.
