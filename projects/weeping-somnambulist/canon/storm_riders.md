# Weeping Somnambulist: Storm Riders

## What They Are

Storm riders are resonant structures in ley line surge energy. Not persistent entities — standing waves in fluid dynamics, like Chladni figures in cymatics. Sound creates visible patterns in material; surge creates visible patterns in the Deep. Storm riders are those patterns given form.

They do not persist between surges because the conditions that generate them do not persist. The form that can be encountered only exists while the surge is running. The Architects failed to study them because they were trying to hold a standing wave in a jar.

During a surge they are both conditions and creatures. Between surges they are neither.

---

## Why Confluences and Storms Attract Them

### The Upwelling Mechanic

In ocean ecology, confluences and turbulence create upwellings — where currents collide, deep material gets carried toward the surface. A ley line confluence is a permanent psychic upwelling: old, dense, compressed material from deep layers mixing upward into shallower ones. For creatures that feed on freshly displaced material, a confluence is a permanent mild feeding ground. During a surge, when flows fall out of phase and collide, the upwelling intensifies enormously. The storm is the feast.

### The Piezoelectric Signature

The Budapest confluence runs through crystalline geological formations under geothermal pressure. Piezoelectricity is generated when crystalline material is compressed — quartz oscillators, bone under load. When ley lines surge through crystalline rock, they produce a specific electromagnetic signature: pressure translating into charge.

Storm riders are attracted to that charge specifically — the intersection of geological pressure and psychic current. The Architects' earliest instruments were electrometers adapted from atmospheric electricity research, accidentally the best storm rider detectors available. They were measuring piezoelectric activity as a proxy for psychic pressure. They didn't know what they were detecting.

**Operational consequence:** Caerulite harvest sites and storm rider feeding sites substantially overlap. The Indigo Fungus clusters in areas of high piezoelectric activity — the same geological signature that draws the riders. You can map one by mapping the other.

### The Budapest Wound

The Eye's separation from TSG produced chronic low-grade disruption at this site for 150 years — not a natural upwelling cycle, an open wound constantly generating active, unresolved material. Storm riders have been drawn here continuously, not just during surges.

The Blooming was extraordinary for them.

Post-reintegration, the desynchronization period — all ley lines falling out of the rhythm the Eye's entrainment pulse maintained for 150 years — will be the richest feeding event since the wound opened. The crew will not be ready for how many come.

## The Pseudoscience Frame

**Odic force (Reichenbach, 1845)** — Reichenbach worked extensively with crystal formations, claimed sensitives saw blue-white luminescence from them under pressure, that Od flowed along the same channels as electromagnetic force. He was reaching toward something real: the piezoelectric charge at a confluence under surge conditions produces exactly what he described. The Architects inherited his framework and used it as their initial classification — "Odic emanations," "surge manifestations." Clinical, wrong-framed, but recognizing the phenomenon.

**Cymatics / Chladni figures** — Ernst Chladni demonstrated that sound creates geometric patterns in vibrating material. The Architects knew this work — it informed their understanding of the heptagonal geometry's acoustic properties. Storm riders are the living analog: resonant patterns in surge energy. The cymatics frame is why Architect instruments could detect them but not capture them. You can observe a Chladni figure; you cannot detach it from the vibrating plate.

**Atmospheric electricity** — the Victorian research context: ball lightning, electrical phenomena in living organisms, galvanism. The Architects' first encounter with storm riders was probably framed through this lens — a form of psychic ball lightning, a surge-born electrical manifestation. The instruments used to study atmospheric electricity were the prototypes for what became the Hum-reading apparatus.

## The Folk Frame

**Szépasszony** — Hungarian: "the Beautiful Woman." A storm spirit encountered at threshold times: noon, midnight, crossroads, the moment between states. Beautiful and dangerous not as a design but as a nature. Not a lure — the Anglers use beauty instrumentally. Storm riders simply are what they are, the way a storm is beautiful.

The folk tradition has a negotiation protocol. The garabonciás — wandering scholars who learned one thing more than permitted — knew how to manage encounters. The táltos tradition has its own version. These protocols work not because they give standing with the riders but because they reduce the practitioner's electromagnetic and psychic signal to something the riders don't need to route around.

**Lorcan's tradition** has a warden protocol for surge events at confluences. It works accidentally correctly — the ink marks him as boundary-adjacent, which the riders' surge-following logic reads as part of the current rather than foreign to it. His tradition understood the outcome without fully understanding the mechanism.

## What the Architects Got Wrong

They tried to hold storm riders between surges. This is why they failed: there is nothing to hold between surges. The riders don't go dormant, don't retreat, don't hide. The form that can be encountered doesn't exist in calm conditions. Their Ghost Files contain what the Eye perceived during storms — the only sustained observational record. Post-reintegration, that archive is gone.

Their clinical terminology: "resonant emanations," "surge manifestations," "Odic condensate events." They classified accurately and misunderstood what the classification meant.

## What Ama Knows

Through direct contact rather than any framework:

**They are not predators.** No threat-direction, no targeting. They are following something she happens to be near. She feels the distinction between a creature that wants her and a creature following a signal she's adjacent to — clearly, without needing to name it.

**They are old.** Pre-narrative, pre-human-layer old. The kind of age her Belong stance can still meet because belonging is pre-linguistic. She recognizes the quality from deep zones. Storm riders have it.

**They are geological as much as psychic.** When she reads the pool before a dive, she feels the rock as alive in a specific register — not plant-alive, not microbial-alive, geological-alive. Slow, compressive, crystalline. Storm riders feel like that register made mobile. The connection between the geological charge and the riders is obvious to her, not a discovery.

**The standing wave recognition.** She doesn't have the physics terminology but knows experientially what the Architects couldn't accept: between surges, nothing is there to find. Her stance doesn't try to hold things — she receives what's present. Between surges the riders simply aren't present. There's no gap to bridge.

**What she could tell Lorcan:** His protocol is accidentally correct. The ink marks him as part of the current rather than foreign to it — the riders don't need to route around him. He's doing it right. He just doesn't know why. She does.
