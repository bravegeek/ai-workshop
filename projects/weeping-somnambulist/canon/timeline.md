# Timeline — The Weeping Somnambulist

Working document. Dates are relative to IO's arrival at the Institute.

---

## Year 0 — Arrival

IO turns 25. A letter from a Vienna law firm barely finds her after grad
school — three forwarding addresses. She calls Nia. Nia says: *"Have you
been to Budapest?"*

They arrive with IO's documents and Nia's sense of direction.

Zoltán meets them at the gate. His family has been expecting someone with
that name. He takes them inside. IO catalogues the first floor. Nia finds
the basement stairs.

**Ren joins within the first few months.** The unlikely third musketeer.
Nia loves her immediately. IO learns to trust Nia's instinct and comes to
appreciate Ren's analytical nature on its own terms. The three of them
begin exploring together — not working, exploring. The distinction matters.

**Year 0, April 30th — First Walpurgisnacht.**
The gathering has been held at this address longer than the building.
Zoltán's family prepares as they always do. Lorcan arrives as he always
does — Budapest is a primary node, he's attended for decades. He meets
the three women. He clocks Nia in the elevator when she says she's
especially excited about the ley line storm. He wants to know them
better. Pressing business pulls him away. He files them and leaves.

---

## Years 0–2 — The Exploration Period

IO, Nia, and Ren exploring together. Lorcan occasionally in Budapest.
The Kovács family on the floors above. The school lease renewed — Nia
talks IO out of letting it lapse.

They live modestly. The Mordaunt trust sustains them. IO's previous life
left resources. Nobody is counting carefully because nobody needs to.

The richest period that never gets told directly — or told in fragments,
through memory and implication. Candidate for expanded treatment as

territory to develop:

- First dives together: what they find, what goes wrong, what they
  don't expect. The vocabulary being built in real time.
- Artifact evenings: three people around something they don't understand,
  arguing about what it is, occasionally being right.
- The school lease confrontation and Nia's Tuesday class beginning.
- Early Walpurgisnacht: the gathering, the community, the politics of
  inheriting this address.
- The bond forming — not through declaration but through accumulated
  shared experience of difficult places.

**Year 2 inflection: Lorcan joins after the oubliette.**
Before Lorcan, the dives are brave and under-anchored. After, they get
serious. Lorcan's warden knowledge fills the gaps they didn't know they
had — where the danger actually lives, how to read the Deep's immune
response, what an oubliette looks like from the inside versus the outside,
protocol for coming back. The Resonance Collection's first crude
iterations sharpen into something more deliberate. They go deeper because
they now know how to do it safely. The exploration becomes more ambitious
precisely as it becomes more disciplined.

**The bond between them — Nia, Ren, IO — is forged here.** Not by
design. By shared experience of something extraordinary, and the specific
trust of people who have held each other in difficult places.

---

## Year 2 — Lorcan Joins

Lorcan finds them in an oubliette. They've gone further than they should
without an anchor. He does the job — hauls them back, holds the line,
manages what needs managing. Afterward he doesn't leave. The question of
whether he's staying stops being a question.

He has the Warden's specific context: he knows what the Eye and Caerulite
did to the Architects because he was there. He is now watching a second
crew. He stays in part because someone needs to be present for the moment
when it matters. He hasn't said what he knows yet. He is waiting for the
moment someone can hear it.

---

## Years 2–4 — The Drift

The exploration continues with Lorcan. The vocabulary is fuller now, the
dives more confident, the artifact understanding deepening. Lorcan's
warden knowledge fills in gaps the three of them couldn't fill alone.

Somewhere in here the exploration starts drifting toward something more
purposeful. IO's mind doing what IO's mind does — she sees what the Deep
holds and begins to understand what could be done with it. Nobody marks
the line crossing. One day they're exploring and the next they're doing
something that looks like work. The shift is gradual and then it isn't.

---

## Year 3–4 — Ama Arrives

Ama follows thin places. Budapest is the loudest confluence in the
European network. She was always going to end up here eventually.

She shows up at the gate. Nia answers. They recognize something in each
other without being able to name it — two people who belong to the Deep
rather than working it, arrived there by completely different routes.

She joins not because she's recruited but because she belongs here and
everyone immediately knows it. IO is the last to know it and the first
to understand the specific gap Ama fills.

Ama arrives late enough that the drift has begun — she sees both what
the exploration was and what it's becoming. She keeps a private tally.

---

## Years 4–5 — The Full Crew

All of them together. The one period where the crew is complete.

IO, Nia, Ren, Lorcan, Ama. The building full. The Kovács family above.
The school running. The courtyard garden that Ama and Erzsébet plant
together. Tuesday afternoons. Walpurgisnacht with a full crew for the
first time.

The work is still closer to exploration than operation. They live
modestly. The artifacts they're bringing back are stranger and more
personal than anything the later operation produces — they're finding
things they weren't looking for.

This period ends.

---

## Year 5 — Nia Disappears

IO is 29-30.

The communion goes somewhere it doesn't come back from. The Eye holds
the thread. Nia doesn't return in the way anyone knows how to reach.

IO cannot save her. Didn't see it coming, couldn't prevent it, couldn't
reverse it. IO, who builds apparatus for every contingency, who engineers
around every limitation, was completely powerless at the one moment that
mattered.

The building changes quality. The way it changed the day she arrived,
reversed.

---

## Years 5–11 — The Machine

IO builds.

Not for money. Not for reputation. She couldn't save Nia. She will never
be powerless again. The extraction work, the favor network, the security
architecture, the Resonance Collection at full development — all of it is
IO making herself ungovernable by grief by making herself ungovernable by
everything else. Control is the only language she has for the feeling
underneath it. She speaks it fluently.

The not-looking is built into the architecture. If she stops building
she has to feel what she's building on top of.

**Why the crew stays:**

*Ren* — the Institute is the only place in the world where her specific
mind has the right tools. She lost Nia too. She and IO hold the grief
differently but in the same building. The operation produces knowledge
about the Deep that wouldn't exist otherwise. She files her discomfort
and stays.

*Lorcan* — the Itch keeps him in Budapest. The crew is the closest thing
to a clan function he's had in decades. And someone needs to be present
for the moment IO can hear what he knows. He hasn't said it yet.

*Ama* — the confluence is still here. Her tradition is patience. She
watched the grief turn into construction in real time and she is waiting
for the moment IO is ready to hear something different. She has time.

Six years. The machine runs. IO is 35-36.

## Year 11 — Story Present

The Blooming coming.

IO at operational peak — wealth, power, emptiness. The crew in place.
The Kovács family above. Lili humming something without a name. Barnaby
near the basement stairs.

The Eye's gradient shifting. Ren filing readings she can't source-
attribute. Something in the Ghost Files moving.

Somewhere in the Vault, an artifact's cage is open.

The story begins.
