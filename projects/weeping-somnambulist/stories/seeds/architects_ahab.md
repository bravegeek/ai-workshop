# The Architects — The Hunt and the Catch (Seed)

Unexplored backstory for how the Eye came to be in the Cistern. Not yet committed to canon. Develop before writing into `canon/craft/technology.md` or `canon/architects.md`.

---

## The Core Irony

The Architects thought they hunted the Eye. The Eye was already coming toward Budapest.

Ahab harpoons the whale. The whale had already stopped swimming. The chains were added afterward — to contain something that had already chosen to stop. Every assumption the Architects made about what they'd accomplished was wrong in the same direction: they attributed to skill what was happening anyway.

---

## The Hunt

A founding Architect — the Mordaunt who formalized the operation — tracked the Eye through the Deep for years. Possibly a decade. The entity left a wake: oubliettes slightly reorganized after it passed through, ley currents displaced, artifacts in its path subtly changed. A practiced tracker could follow that trail. An obsessive would.

**What Ahab saw in the oubliettes:** The fragment moving through concentrated experience nodes with apparent purpose — pausing, absorbing, moving on. Not predatory. Not random. The Architects had no framework for archival collection; they read it as a Leviathan feeding. The behavior was frightening because it was purposeful and because they couldn't predict it.

**What the entity actually was doing:** Collection cycles. Sampling the most significant concentration points in the network. It was doing exactly what TSG sent it to do. Budapest was always the destination.

**The name Ahab:** Self-applied or given. The journals — which Barnaby still has, or knows the location of — use it. Whether as irony or identification is unclear. They knew the obsession was consuming them.

---

## The Trap

The Architects couldn't carry a TSG fragment in a Witness — not the whole thing. What they could do was get a *lock*: a Witness carrying a precise attunement to the fragment's position. A thread back through the labyrinth.

The fragment, following chemotaxis toward Budapest, would naturally track toward that attunement when the storm-charged pool began drawing it. Standard development mechanics — but the object being developed was a piece of a sleeping god.

**What they expected:** Another failed development. Or a partial one. Or something unstable and large, like their pre-Eye over-developments. They probably had containment plans. They probably thought they were being careful.

**What happened:** The development stopped cleanly. For the first time in Architect history, a storm-pool development ended without overrun. No pool-residue absorption, no runaway pattern expansion. Fixed, precise, done.

The Eye provided its own stop bath. Its own Lethe-Light — expressing through the development process — fixed the interference pattern. The fragment self-anchored at 80 metres in the Budapest confluence.

**The first controlled artifact development in Architect history was an artifact that developed itself.**

The Architects thought they'd solved the stop-bath problem. They had not. The Eye had solved it for them, once, because it needed to be exactly here.

---

## The Chains

Added after. Addressing lateral drift from an entity that was never trying to drift laterally. A solution to the wrong problem, built in good faith by people who had no way to know the entity was already done moving.

The chains are the clearest symbol of the whole Architect misread: professional, well-engineered, completely beside the point.

---

## Barnaby

He was there. He was present when the Architects tracked the entity, when they developed it, when the Light stopped the process. He is the only living witness to what actually happened in that cistern in 1887.

Whether he understood at the time what he was watching is unresolved. Whether he's made the connection now is a story question. Whether he ever told Ahab — before or after — what the entity actually was is the question that matters most for his arc.

He has the journals. Or knows where they are. The journals contain Ahab's full account: the years of tracking, the failed development attempts, the catch, and a completely confident misreading of what they'd accomplished.
