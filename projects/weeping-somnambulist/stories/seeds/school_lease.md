# The Lease

Early period. IO and Nia are still finding the shape of what they have —
what the building is, what the basement holds, what the confluence means.
The school's lease is up for renewal. IO doesn't renew it.

Or starts not to.

---

Nia finds her in the study with the paperwork.

IO's argument is reasonable: the artifacts they're bringing in could be
dangerous. Children in the building is irresponsible. The school has had
thirty-seven years here; they're not being put out, they'll find somewhere
else, IO will help them find somewhere else.

Nia says: everyone in the city already knows this campus is kísértetjárta.
Has for a hundred years. A little more isn't going to raise eyebrows. And
the school is cover — local families, normal comings and goings, a reason
the building looks like what it's always been. IO's operational logic works
against her here and she knows it.

What Nia actually means is: she likes being part of the community. She has
learned Erzsébet's name and Zoltán's father's name and where Dóra goes when
she wants to be alone. This is home. She is not leaving and she is not
making it a facility.

IO looks at her for a moment.

"Are you teaching a class?"

Nia shrugs.

IO looks at the paperwork. "How long?"

Nia thinks about it. "Since March."

It's October.

IO sets the paperwork down. She doesn't say the school is staying — it's
already decided, has been since the shrug, and they both know it. She looks
out at the courtyard and something in her chest is slightly less tight than
it was.

Nia is teaching ear training on Tuesday afternoons. She described it to the
school director as "creative listening." The director took this to mean music
theory. The children learn to pay attention with their whole bodies. They
don't know why they love it. They just do.

---

Six years later, the knot is back. The courtyard is the same. Tuesday
afternoons are not.
