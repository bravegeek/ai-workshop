# The Weeping Somnambulist — Story Outline

---

## Arc 1: The Machine

**Frame:** Six years post-Nia. IO at operational peak. The Institute running clean. Nia present only as absence — what the campus still holds of her: Tuesday afternoon light in the courtyard, the garden she kept, the school that persisted because she built it to outlast her.

---

### Act I — Operations

**Situation:**
- The extraction operation at full capacity. IO controls access to the Psychosphere, controls the Institute, controls the Eye.
- Nia understood through what remains: the garden, the students, the rhythms nobody canceled.
- Ren runs the infrastructure. Ama runs the school. IO runs everything above that.

**Beat 1 — Unknown Practitioner**
- Routine extraction job. Fresh tracks in the target's layer — technique IO doesn't recognize, signature she can't attribute.
- Someone outside the Institute is working the Psychosphere. Not rogue, not naive — *capable*.
- First crack in the frame: the world outside the Institute is more complicated than IO controls.

**Key question:** Who else has access, and how long have they had it?

---

### Act II — The Gathering

**Beat 2 — Walpurgisnacht**
- The annual practitioner gathering. Politics, inheritance, old debts. IO attends as Institute director — a role Nia held differently.
- What IO has inherited by holding the Institute: obligations, resentments, alliances she didn't negotiate.
- Barnaby increasingly impossible to ignore. His angle unclear. His interest in IO specific.
- The Eye's readings during the gathering show something anomalous. Ren can't source-attribute it. It doesn't match any known practitioner signature.

**Key questions:** What is the Eye catching that has no known source? What does Barnaby want?

---

### Act III — The Blooming

**Beat 3 — Collection Cycle Completes**
- The Eye's accumulation reaches threshold. The Blooming begins.
- IO tries to solve it with the machine — increased extraction, diagnostic runs, containment protocols. Standard operational response to a non-standard event.
- The machine isn't calibrated for this. The Blooming is not an operational problem.
- The Eye's logic field collapses. Everything built around it stops working.
- IO left without the apparatus she's run for six years.

**Key question:** What happens to IO when the machine stops?

---

## Arc 2: The Forest

**Frame:** Post-Blooming. The Institute changed. IO forced still — too exhausted to run, too stripped to operate. Grief has been waiting. There's nothing left to outrun it with.

---

### Act I — Rebuild

**Beat 4 — Institutional Rebuild**
- Ren leads the reconstruction. Practical, methodical, his domain.
- The Institute becoming something different from what IO built it into — closer, maybe, to what Nia intended.
- IO peripheral to this process. She can observe but not direct.

**Beat 5 — The Jumper Moves**
- Post-Blooming the cage logic has changed. The Jumper has been watching Ren — specifically, consistently.
- It makes Ren an offer. The offer is genuine. That's what makes it dangerous.

**Key question:** What does the Jumper want from Ren, and what is Ren capable of wanting back?

---

### Act II — Reception

**Beat 6 — Ama and IO**
- Ama works with IO directly. Slow, patient erosion of the extraction instinct.
- IO learning to receive rather than extract. Patience and non-interpretation — Ama's tradition, the structural inverse of IO's practice.
- The imposing instinct doesn't go quietly.

**Beat 7 — First Communion Attempt**
- IO goes into the forest without looking for anything. First time in her life.
- Costs her more effort than the hardest extraction she ever ran.
- She gets the tiniest taste of it — not skill, barely a foothold.
- Nia would have done this before breakfast.

**Key question:** What does IO find when she stops extracting?

---

### Act III — Resolution

**Beat 8 — The Ghost Files**
- IO, now capable of receiving, accesses what Nia experienced — the Eye's archive of the communion sessions before the Blooming.
- Not Nia back. Not reunion. Understanding.
- The loop closes: IO learns what Nia was doing in the forest all those Tuesday afternoons.

**Beat 9 — IO Present**
- The Institute in its new shape. IO present without a mission for the first time.
- The garden is still there.

**Key question:** What does IO do with herself when she's not running the machine?

---

## Standing Questions (Both Arcs)

- The unknown practitioner from Act 1 — who, and do they surface again?
- Barnaby's angle — what does he know about the Blooming that IO doesn't?
- The Eye post-Blooming — what form does it take, if any?
- Ren's answer to the Jumper's offer.
