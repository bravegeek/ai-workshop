# The Descent: Zones & Motivations

## I. The Bathymetry of Sleep (Zonal Changes)
As a Dreamwalker descends, the "Physics of Thought" changes. These are the reliable indicators of depth.

### Zone 1: The Surface (The R.E.M. Crust)
*   **Depth:** 0 - 100m (Psychic Equivalent)
*   **The Look:** Hyper-real, chaotic, and glitchy. Like flipping channels on a 4K TV.
*   **The Sound:** White noise, snippets of conversation, traffic, radio static.
*   **Physics:** Looney Tunes logic. You can fly if you believe you can. Doors lead to random places.
*   **The Danger:** Low. Mostly embarrassing or confusing.
*   **NPCs:** "Extras." Faceless, blurry people who fill crowds.

### Zone 2: The Shallows (The Archetypal Twilight)
*   **Depth:** 100m - 1,000m
*   **The Look:** Cinematic and cohesive. The lighting is always "Golden Hour" or "Blue Hour." The resolution shifts from digital video to **Oil Painting**. Textures are rich and exaggerated.
*   **The Sound:** Music. Not songs, but *scores*. A constant, emotional soundtrack that dictates the mood.
*   **Physics:** Narrative Logic. You can't just "fly"; you need wings. Doors lead to thematically connected places (e.g., a school door leads to a prison).
*   **The Danger:** Medium. Getting caught in "Melodrama Loops" (emotional quicksand).
*   **NPCs:** "The Cast." Distinct characters. Often amalgams of people you know (e.g., a teacher who is also your mother).

### Zone 3: The Thermocline (The Barrier / The Babel Zone)
*   **Depth:** The Transition Layer
*   **The Phenomenon:** The temperature (emotional warmth) drops instantly.
*   **The Shift:** **Language breaks down.** Words on signs scramble into alien glyphs. Spoken language becomes sound-color (Synesthesia). You cannot read or speak here; you must *communicate via Intent*.
*   **Visuals:** Geometric fractals intrude on reality. The world looks like it's buffering or rendering.
*   **The Test:** This filters out the tourists. If your mind cannot handle the loss of language, you wake up screaming (The Kick).

### Zone 4: The Deep (The Midnight Zone)
*   **Depth:** 1,000m+
*   **The Look:** Minimalist, high-contrast, surreal. **The Void Aesthetic.** Objects are isolated in darkness—a single chair in an infinite black room.
*   **The Sound:** The "Thrum." A low-frequency vibration (like a massive engine or a heartbeat) that rattles your bones.
*   **Physics:** **Symbolic Gravity.** Objects with "Heavy Meaning" (Guilt, Grief, Secrets) are physically impossible to lift. You can walk on walls if your perspective changes.
*   **The Danger:** High. Ego Death. Forgetting who you are. Entities hunt here.
*   **NPCs:** "The Avatars." Entities wearing human skins. They speak in riddles or absolute truths.

### Zone 5: The Abyssal (The Silt)
*   **Depth:** The Bottom
*   **The Look:** Monochromatic grey ash. The graveyard of forgotten things.
*   **The Sensation:** Crushing pressure. Every movement requires immense willpower.
*   **The Reality:** Here, **Metaphor becomes Literal.** If you say "I'm falling apart," your limbs will actually detach.

## II. Why We Dive: Motivations for Descent
Beyond the Lantern Crew's espionage, there are distinct types of "Divers" who risk the Deep.

### 1. The Brokers (Data-Miners / Spymasters)
*   **Goal:** **Information Supremacy & Leverage.**
*   **The Archetype:** **IO (The Vector).**
*   **The Mission:** To map the "Dark Data." Brokers treat the Deep as a vault of blackmail material ("Kompromat") and dangerous truths. They dive to extract secrets from powerful minds or to "bury" secrets that threaten stability.
*   **The Philosophy:** "The waking world is built on lies. The Dream is where the ledger is kept."
*   **The Risk:** **"The Glare."** Knowing too much makes you a target for both human enemies and the Entities who hoard secrets.

### 2. The Muse-Hunters (Inspiration Theft)
*   **Goal:** To find the "Pure Idea."
*   **The Theory:** All great art, inventions, and melodies exist in the Collective Unconscious *before* they are discovered.
*   **The Mission:** Dive to the Shallows or the Deep to catch a "Spark"—a melody that hasn't been written, a mathematical proof that hasn't been solved.
*   **The Risk:** "The Icarus Burn." Ideas in the Deep are radioactive. Bringing a "Pure Idea" back to reality without filtering it can drive the artist insane (the "Mad Genius" trope).

### 3. The Retrievers (Rescue Ops)
*   **Goal:** To save the "Lost."
*   **The Mission:** People in comas, catatonic states, or those trapped by Entity-Curses are often "stuck" in a Loop in the Deep. Retrievers go in to find their Avatar and guide them back to the Surface/Waking World.
*   **The Risk:** The "Drowning." The Retriever might get caught in the same loop and never wake up.

### 4. The Pilgrims (Transcendence)
*   **Goal:** Immortality / Ego Death.
*   **The Mission:** Cultists or philosophers who believe the Waking World is the illusion. They dive to the Abyssal to dissolve their consciousness into the Silt, hoping to become one with the collective (or to ascend and become an Entity).
*   **The Risk:** They usually succeed. (Death of the body, absorption of the soul).
