# The Deep & Ley Line Mechanics

## I. The Deep (The Abyssal Void)

The Deep is the "basement" of the collective unconscious. It is where things go when they are too heavy to be remembered, but too powerful to be deleted.

### 1. Geography of the Void
The Deep is not a land; it is a **pressure zone**.
*   **The Silt**: The "floor" of the dream world is composed of billions of forgotten secrets, ground down into a fine, grey powder. Walking on it releases "memory-gas"—step on a patch, and you might suddenly taste your grandmother's cooking or feel the fear of a car crash that happened in 1995.
*   **The Trenches**: Rifts in the Silt where "Dead Data" flows. These are the graveyards of dead religions, fallen empires, and obsolete languages.
*   **The Gravity**: Down here, emotional weight is literal weight. A "Guilt" complex can weigh as much as a tank. Explorers move slowly, as if walking underwater.

### 2. Entity Ecology (The Deep Fauna)
Entities here are not "creatures"; they are **complex autonomous systems**.

*   **The Leviathans (The Silent Ones)**: Massive, slow-moving shapes that resemble deep-sea gigantism mixed with abstract art. They are "World-Eaters"—they consume entire abandoned dreamscapes to recycle the data.
    *   *Interaction*: Generally passive unless provoked. If you touch one, you risk being "archived" (absorbed into its mass).
*   **The Anglers (Lure-Lights)**: Predatory thoughts that manifest as something you desperately want. A lost loved one, a door to a safe house, a pile of gold.
    *   *Mechanism*: They dangle the "Lure" in the dark. When you get close, they snap shut, trapping your consciousness in a "Loop" (a recurring nightmare) while they feed on your distress.
*   **The Mycelium (The Rot)**: A fungal-like network that grows on stagnant secrets. It connects things that shouldn't be connected (e.g., linking a childhood fear of spiders to a fear of public speaking, creating a hybrid phobia).

## II. Ley Lines (The Data Streams)

Ley Lines are the "Fiber Optics" of the Psychosphere—rivers of high-velocity information that connect human minds across the globe.

### 1. Structure & Flow
*   **The Current**: A Ley Line looks like a rushing river of liquid light (Code/Starlight).
*   **Content**: They carry the "Active" data of humanity—current conversations, trending emotions, shared cultural moments.
*   **The Tides**:
    *   **High Tide**: When the world is awake and active. The current is fast, dangerous, and bright.
    *   **Low Tide**: When the world sleeps. The current slows, widens, and becomes "navigable." This is when Dreamwalkers travel.

### 2. Interaction with Reality (The Bleed)
Ley Lines map roughly to human population density and emotional intensity, but they are not 1:1.

*   **Nodes (Confluences)**: Places where lines intersect. In reality, these are often "Haunted" places, or places of high creativity/madness.
    *   *Examples*: A busy stock exchange, a frantic hospital ER, an ancient temple, or **The Cistern**.
*   **The Cistern's Role**: The Cistern is an artificial **"Divert Valve."** The Lantern Crew uses their tech to "bend" a Ley Line slightly off-course, pooling the data into their reservoir. This allows them to "sift" for specific secrets without entering the main torrent.
*   **Burst Events (Poltergeist Phenomena)**:
    *   If a Ley Line gets "clogged" (a blockage of trauma or repressed data), pressure builds.
    *   **The Rupture**: The line bursts into reality. Objects move, electronics fry, people hallucinate.
    *   *Cleanup*: IO's "Architect of Silence" persona is often hired to "fix" these events (covering them up as gas leaks or electrical faults).

## III. The Physics of Interaction (Dream vs. Flesh)

### 1. The Somatic Bridge
*   **Rule**: "The Mind leads, the Body follows."
*   **Damage**:
    *   **Psychic Damage**: If you get "cut" in a dream, your physical body develops a psychosomatic welt or bruise.
    *   **Severance**: If your dream-self is killed, your physical body enters a vegetative state (The Hollow).
*   **Sync-Ratio**: The percentage of your consciousness projected into the dream.
    *   **Lucid (10-30%)**: Safe. You watch the dream like a movie.
    *   **Deep Dive (80-90%)**: Dangerous. You *are* the dream. High risk of "drowning" (forgetting your physical body exists).

### 2. Artifact Translation
*   **Why it's Hard**: Dream objects are made of *meaning*, not matter.
*   **The Anchor Principle**: To bring an item back, you must "convince" the universe it exists.
    *   *Method*: You need a physical "Seed" (The Crucible Method) that shares a *sympathetic resonance* with the dream object.
    *   *Example*: To bring back a "Sword of Truth," you might need to use an antique judge's gavel as the seed. The "Truth" concept bonds to the "Judgment" object.
