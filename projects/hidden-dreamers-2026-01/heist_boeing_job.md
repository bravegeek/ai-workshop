# Mission Profile: The "Blue-Sky" Job

## 1. The Contract
*   **Client:** **Aero-Dyne Systems** (The Corp).
*   **Target:** **Dr. Aris Thorne**, Lead Engineer at **Zephyr-Works**.
*   **Objective:** Extract the **"Atmospheric Stabilizer Algorithm"** (Project Blue-Sky).
*   **Payout:** 50,000 Credits (Liquid) + 2 High-Grade Psyloc-Cores.
*   **The Real Goal (IO's Twist):** Sabotage Aero-Dyne’s prototype to prevent a dangerous monopoly, while shorting their stock for Enclave funding.

---

## 2. Reconnaissance (The Profile)
*   **Subject:** Dr. Thorne is overworked, anxious, and deeply resentful of "Corporate Oversight."
*   **Psych-Profile:** Obsessive-Compulsive tendencies. Fears "Collapse" and "Incompetence."
*   **Dream Signature:** High-Altitude Anxiety. Structural instability.

## 3. The Dreamscape: "The Infinite Scaffold"
*   **Zone:** **The Shallows (Zone 2)** drifting into **The Thermocline (Zone 3)** due to stress.
*   **Aesthetic:** A limitless, rusted construction site in the clouds. The wind is deafening.
*   **The Logic:**
    *   **The Tower:** Thorne is building a tower that never ends.
    *   **The Suits:** Faceless men in grey suits circle like vultures (birds), screeching for "Results."
    *   **The Symbol (The MacGuffin):** The **Golden Rivet**. Thorne holds a single, glowing golden rivet in his hand. He believes if he places it, the tower stands. If he drops it, the world ends.
    *   **The Rivet = The Algorithm.**

## 4. The Infiltration Plan
*   **IO's Role:** **"The Union Foreman."**
    *   *Costume:* High-vis vest, hard hat, clipboard.
    *   *Vibe:* Authoritative, calm, "Blue Collar Savior."
    *   *Strategy:* He cannot *take* the rivet (Thorne will wake up or the dream will attack). He must convince Thorne that the "Shift is over" and he needs to "Check the tool back into the locker."
*   **Barnaby's Role (The Ghost Dog):** **"The Site Mascot."**
    *   *Appearance:* A scrappy, oil-stained Terrier with a tool-belt collar.
    *   *Function:* **The Spotter.** The "Suits" (Vultures) try to swoop down and steal the rivet. Barnaby barks to warn IO of incoming "Corporate Interference" (Nightmare Enemies).

## 5. The Heist Execution
1.  **Entry:** IO drops onto the scaffold. The wind howls.
2.  **Contact:** IO approaches Thorne, who is weeping, trying to hammer the rivet into a cloud.
3.  **The Pitch:** "Dr. Thorne! Union rules. Mandatory break. Hand over the equipment."
4.  **The Conflict:** The Vultures (The Suits) attack, screeching "DEADLINE! DEADLINE!"
5.  **The Snatch:** Barnaby chases the birds away. IO swaps the **Golden Rivet** for a **"Dummy Rivet"** (a rusted bolt he brought in).
6.  **The Exit:** IO blows a whistle. "Shift's over!" The scaffold dissolves.

## 6. The "Robin Hood" Laundering
1.  **The Delivery:** IO delivers the **Golden Rivet's Data** to Aero-Dyne.
    *   *The Sabotage:* He edits the code. The math works *perfectly* in simulation, but has a "Heat-Sink Error" that only triggers after 4 hours of continuous runtime (i.e., during their live public demo).
2.  **The Leak:** IO sends the original, clean code to a "Public Domain" patent office, timed to release *after* Aero-Dyne's demo fails.
3.  **The Profit:** The Enclave shorts Aero-Dyne stock. The money buys new "Dream-Filters" for the comatose ward.
