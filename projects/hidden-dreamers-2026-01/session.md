# Hidden Dreamers: Universe Foundation

## Session Summary
This session established the metaphysical and social foundation for "Hidden Dreamers," a project exploring a universe of dreamwalking. We moved through a progression from world mechanics to psychological rules, ending with a faction-based narrative engine.

## Context
The goal was to build a cohesive world where dreamwalking is the central mechanic, starting with the nature of the dreamworld itself and scaling up to societal conflict.

## Expert Role
**The Metaphysical Architect**: Adopted to ensure the "physics" and "ethics" of the dream world are consistent and logically sound.

## Core World Mechanics
- **The Collective Construct (The Psychosphere)**: The dreamworld is a biological internet sustained by the consciousness of all living things, including humans and animals.
- **Vertical Geography**:
    - **The Surface**: Individual daily dreams (chaotic, fleeting).
    - **The Shallows**: The shared subconscious (archetypes, symbols, common ground).
    - **The Deep (The Abyssal Void)**: An indigenous, ancient eldritch ocean that existed before consciousness.
- **The Sanity Mechanic**: Depth = Truth + Madness. Diving deeper provides greater understanding of reality but risks crushing the mind under alien weight.

## The Faction Triad
The "Hidden Dreamers" are split into three primary groups with conflicting agendas:
1. **The Naturals (The Immune System)**: Born with the gift. They see the Psychosphere as sacred biology and act as protectors/gatekeepers.
2. **The Techs (The Miners)**: Use neural-tech and drugs to force entry. They view the realm as a resource to be harvested or a system to be hacked.
3. **The Ritualists (The Worshippers)**: Use occult methods to communicate with the Deep. They seek transcendence or communion with the Eldritch entities.

## Proposed Research Plan
- **Mythology**: Comparing Australian Dreamtime and Tibetan Dream Yoga.
- **Ecosystems**: Deep-sea biology and animal sensory systems.
- **Psychology**: Jungian archetypes and sleep paralysis mechanics.
- **Politics**: Analysis of three-way conflict in sci-fi/fantasy.

## Next Steps

- Establish the "Threshold" mechanics (how one moves between Surface, Shallows, and Deep).
- Flesh out the first major conflict between the Techs and the Naturals.
