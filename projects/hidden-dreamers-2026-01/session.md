# Hidden Dreamers: Universe Foundation

## Session Summary
This session established the metaphysical and social foundation for "Hidden Dreamers," a project exploring a universe of dreamwalking. We moved through a progression from world mechanics to psychological rules, ending with a faction-based narrative engine and character profiles for a short story.

## Context
The goal was to build a cohesive world where dreamwalking is the central mechanic, starting with the nature of the dreamworld itself and scaling up to societal conflict and individual character stakes.

## Expert Role
**The Metaphysical Architect**: Adopted to ensure the "physics" and "ethics" of the dream world are consistent and logically sound.

## Core World Mechanics
- **The Collective Construct (The Psychosphere)**: The dreamworld is a biological internet sustained by the consciousness of all living things.
- **Vertical Geography**:
    - **The Surface**: Individual daily dreams.
    - **The Shallows**: Shared subconscious (archetypes).
    - **The Deep (The Abyssal Void)**: Ancient eldritch ocean.
- **The Sanity Mechanic (Pressure)**: Depth = Truth + Madness. Rapid ascent causes "Psychic Bends" (Sleep Paralysis).
- **Bioluminescence as Data**: Light in the Deep is raw information that overwrites the observer's brain.

## The Setting: The Obsidian Needle
- **Anchor Point**: Located beneath a **Metropolis** (The Urban Anchor).
- **The Sky ("The Static Ceiling")**: A roiling storm of neon light representing the collective noise of 20 million dreamers.
- **Structure**: An inverted skyscraper hanging from the city's "surface tension."
- **The Tip ("The Lantern")**: A geodesic sphere of Oneiro-Glass at the bottom, designed to attract and harvest "wild ideas."

## The Scenario: "The Audit of the Damned"
- **The Mystery**: Rig 9 went silent but efficiency spiked.
- **The Truth**: The crew has been "colonized" by a Hadal Entity, forming a Hive Mind.
- **The Twist**: The rig is no longer a drill; it is an **Antenna** broadcasting a signal from the Abyss into the heart of the Metropolis.

## The Cast
1. **Elara Vance (The Canary - Natural)**: High sensitivity; smells lies as sulfur and feels the entity as a migraine. Wants to save the city.
2. **Kaelen Thorne (The Diver - Ritualist)**: Benthic specialist in a runic soul-cage suit. Respects the monsters and seeks the Truth of the Deep.
3. **Proctor IO (The Calculator - Tech)**: A gestalt operative plugged into Corporate AI. Views the "infection" as a monetizable asset/product.
