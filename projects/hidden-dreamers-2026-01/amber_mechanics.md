# Hidden Dreamers: The Amber Bleed & Stasis Mechanics

## I. The Amber Bleed (The Anomaly)
The Amber Bleed is the central ecological crisis of the Deep. It is not a natural phenomenon; it is a **pathology**.

### 1. Origin: The Prism Theory
*   **The Source:** The **Sleeping God** (The Unbroken Tongue).
*   **The Filter:** **Nia Aethelgard** (The Pilot Fish).
*   **The Mechanism:** The God's raw power (Creation/Unity) is being blasted through Nia's human consciousness.
    *   **The Distortion:** Nia's mind cannot handle the voltage. Her trauma, loneliness, and specific memories (Lavender, Childhood Fear) act as a "Lens."
    *   **The Result:** The energy exiting her is not pure "Dream"; it is **Amber Glass**. It is the God's power *calcified* by human grief.

### 2. Properties of Amber (Material Science)
*   **Scent:** Burnt Solder and Lavender.
*   **Visual:** Transparent, warm-gold glass. It glows with a steady, non-flickering light (unlike the chaotic pulses of the Deep).
*   **Durability:** Indestructible by standard Dream-Logic. You cannot "disbelieve" it away. It requires **Resonance Breaking** (Sound) or **Void-Tech** (Glitch-Blade) to shatter.

## II. The Pathology: Stasis (Dream Cancer)

### 1. The Anti-Dream
Dreams are defined by **Fluidity** (Change). Amber is defined by **Stasis** (Permanence).
*   **The Effect:** Amber does not "kill"; it **Preserves**.
*   **Vitrification:** When the Amber Wave touches a dream-structure (e.g., The Fossil Grove), it halts its entropy. The rotting trees become glass statues. They stop dying. They stop changing. They exist forever in that exact second.

### 2. Interaction with Dreamwalkers (The Trap)
*   **The Thought-Loop:** If a Diver touches the Amber (or inhales the pollen/dust):
    *   **Physical:** Their Dream-Body hardens into glass.
    *   **Mental:** They are **indexed** in the exact frame of thought they held at contact.
    *   *Example:* If Thorne touches it while thinking "Fire," he becomes a glass statue eternally locked in the *impulse* to pull the trigger. He is conscious, screaming in his own mind, but unable to complete the synaptic firing.
*   **Rescue:** Freeing someone from Amber requires shattering the glass (Risk of shattering the mind) or finding the "Resonant Frequency" that matches the trapped emotion to liquefy it.

## III. Ecological Impact: The Clogged Drain

### 1. The Tides Stop
The Deep relies on the **Somatic Cycle** (Tides) to wash away the city's psychic waste ("The Silt").
*   **The Blockage:** The Amber is expanding. It acts as a dam, preventing the natural flow of Ley Lines.
*   **The Stagnation:** The Silt is piling up. The Deep is becoming a "Swamp" rather than an "Ocean."

### 2. Surface Symptoms (The City Bleeds)
Because the "Septic System" of the collective unconscious is clogged, the waste is backing up into Reality.
*   **Phase 1: The Deja Vu Plague.** Citizens experience intense, debilitating echoes of "Already Happened."
*   **Phase 2: The Loop Syndrome.** Entire city blocks get stuck in 10-second behavioral loops (Repeating a sentence, walking in a circle).
*   **Phase 3: Mass Insomnia.** The city *cannot sleep* because the Dreamworld is "Full."
    *   *Statistic:* Suicide rates and "Accidental Fugue States" are spiking in districts directly above Amber Zones.

### 3. The Leviathan Response (Starvation)
*   **The Beached Whales:** The Leviathans feed on *flowing* data. They cannot eat Glass.
*   **Behavior Change:** They are no longer passive grazers. They are desperate predators.
*   **The Target:** They are hunting **Dreamwalkers** (Fluid Data sources) and attacking the Amber shell, trying to break it open to get to the "Marrow" (The God) inside.
