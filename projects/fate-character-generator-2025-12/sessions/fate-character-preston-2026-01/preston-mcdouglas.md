# Fate Core Character Sheet

## Character Name: Preston "Null-Pointer" McDouglas

**Player**: (User)
**Campaign**: Sci-Fi / Space Opera
**Refresh**: 3
**Current Fate Points**: 3

---

## Description

A grizzled, cynical ex-Galactic Marine sergeant who has seen far too much. He is heavily scarred and carries himself with a weary authority. His most striking feature is a military-grade cybernetic eye that is permanently stuck in "Gamer Mode," cycling through soft pastels (Pink, Teal, Lime) and occasionally flashing "404 ERROR" in blue light. He smells faintly of ozone and old cigar smoke.

---

## Aspects

### High Concept
**Cynical Ex-Galactic Marine**

*Invoke this when you need to navigate military bureaucracy, handle heavy weapons, endure physical hardship, or use tactical knowledge. It represents your grit and training.*

*Compel this when your cynical nature alienates civilians, when you underestimate an idealistic solution, or when military protocol or past orders restrict your freedom.*

### Trouble
**Flashbacks to the Felinid Uprising (Deathly afraid of felines)**

*Invoke this to react with extreme speed to feline-like threats, to bond with fellow veterans of the Uprising, or when paranoia keeps you alive.*

*Compel this when a harmless cat causes you to freeze, panic, or open fire inappropriately, or when your trauma makes you unstable in a crisis.*

### Phase One: The Admiral's Dirty Secret
**I Know the Secret of the Admiral's "Daughter"**

*Invoke this to identify synthetic beings, to resist deception from high-ranking officials, or to leverage knowledge against the Admiralty.*

*Compel this when the Admiral's agents track you down, when you are trusted with secrets you don't want, or when you hesitate to trust someone who seems "too perfect."*

### Phase Two: Debt to the Scrapyard
**I Owe My Life to a Junk-Heap Pilot**

*Invoke this to get emergency extraction, to find parts or repairs in a scrapyard, or to secure transport from sketchy sources.*

*Compel this when that pilot calls in a dangerous favor, when you're forced to defend their terrible reputation, or when you have to entrust your life to substandard equipment.*

### Phase Three: Veteran's Instincts
**A Soldier's Sixth Sense**

*Invoke this to spot ambushes, sense danger before it happens, read body language in a tense standoff, or wake up instantly.*

*Compel this when you react to a threat that isn't there (paranoia), when you can't relax in a safe environment, or when you treat a friendly surprise as a hostile act.*

### Skill Descriptions

- **Contacts (+4)**: You know every quartermaster, merc bar, and vet clinic in the sector. You call in favors, find black market gear, and know who to bribe.
- **Provoke (+3)**: You have the "drill sergeant voice" down to a science. You scare people into compliance or draw their fire by making them angry.
- **Shoot (+3)**: You don't miss when it counts. Whether it's suppressing fire or a precision shot, you know your way around a blaster.
- **Will (+2)**: You've seen friends die and planets burn. It takes a lot to shake you mentally (except cats).
- **Notice (+2)**: You keep your head on a swivel. Your cybernetic eye might be glitchy, but it catches movement.
- **Physique (+2)**: You're tough, scarred, and can carry a full rucksack for days.
- **Athletics (+1)**: You're not as fast as you used to be, but you know how to duck and cover.
- **Fight (+1)**: Dirty fighting learned in trench warfare. Headbutts and entrenching tools.
- **Lore (+1)**: Knowledge of military history, unit insignia, and standard operating procedures.
- **Drive (+1)**: You can pilot a standard APC or troop transport without crashing (mostly).

## Stunts

### 1. "Listen Up, Nuggets!"
**Because I am a seasoned sergeant**, when I am dealing with military personnel or veterans, I can use **Contacts** instead of **Rapport**.

*This allows you to bark orders and use your rank/reputation to influence soldiers. You don't have to be nice; you just have to be Sarge.*

---

### 2. Covering Fire
**Because I know how to keep heads down**, when I use **Shoot** to Create an Advantage (e.g., *Pinned Down*), I can use the higher of my **Shoot** or **Provoke** skill, and I get an extra free invoke on the aspect if I succeed.

*Great for controlling the battlefield and setting up allies. Use this to lock enemies in place so your heavy hitters can flank them.*

---

### 3. "Sarge's Sixth Sense"
**Because I've survived a hundred warzones**, I get +2 to **Notice** to see an ambush coming or to spot a hidden enemy, provided I am in a combat or high-tension environment.

*Use this to prevent the party from being surprised. You are the early warning system.*

## Backstory Summary

### Origin: The Legendary Sergeant
Preston McDouglas was a legendary Sergeant in the Galactic Marines, known for keeping his squad alive in the most brutal conditions. He wasn't the strongest or the fastest, but he was the smartest and the meanest when it mattered. He built a network of contacts across the service, men and women who owed him their lives.

### Trauma: The Felinid Uprising
His cynicism was cemented during the Felinid Uprising, a brutal conflict on a jungle moon. The guerrilla tactics of the feline natives decimated his platoon. He developed a deep-seated trauma regarding anything feline and a distrust of command for sending them into a meat grinder with bad intel.

### Current Situation: The Fringe
He was forced into "early retirement" after an ill-advised affair with the Admiral's "daughter," who he discovered was actually a high-end synthetic prototype—a secret he still holds. Now, he survives on the fringes of the galaxy, calling in old favors from the men and women who still call him "Sarge." He smells of ozone and old cigar smoke, looking for the next job that pays enough to keep his cybernetic eye running.

## Notes & Reminders

- **Contacts as Social**: Remember your *Listen Up, Nuggets!* stunt allows you to be social without being charming. Use your military connections constantly.
- **Support Gunner**: Your *Covering Fire* is powerful. Don't just attack; set up your allies with free invokes.
- **Cyber-Eye Glitch**: Your eye is a great source of color but also a narrative tool for the GM. It might glitch at the wrong time (Compel) or reveal IR signatures (Invoke).

## Play Styles

Preston can be played in multiple ways depending on what aspects of his concept you emphasize. Here are 4 distinct approaches:

### Style 1: The Squad Leader (Support/Command)

**Core Approach**: You control the battlefield by directing traffic and locking down enemies, acting as the team's anchor.

**Tactics**:
- Use **Covering Fire** (Shoot/Provoke) to place *Pinned Down* aspects on dangerous foes, giving allies free invokes to finish them.
- Use **Contacts** to Create Advantages like *Reinforcements on Standby* or *Intel from HQ* before the fight starts.
- Invoke **Cynical Ex-Galactic Marine** to resist fear effects that would scatter the team.
- Use **Provoke** to draw fire away from wounded allies ("Is that all you got, maggot?!").

**Fate Point Economy**:
- **Spend on**: Invoking aspects to ensure your *Covering Fire* succeeds with style (generating more boosts).
- **Gain from**: Compels on **Flashbacks to the Felinid Uprising** when your caution makes you hesitate to push forward.

**Strengths**: Force multiplication, battlefield control.
**Weaknesses**: Lower raw damage output.

---

### Style 2: The Connected Veteran (Social/Info)

**Core Approach**: You solve problems by knowing the right people and leaning on your reputation, avoiding fair fights whenever possible.

**Tactics**:
- Lean heavily on **Contacts** (+4). Why fight the guard when you served with his uncle?
- Use **"Listen Up, Nuggets!"** to bypass social obstacles that usually require Rapport.
- Create Advantages with **Lore** to recall military protocols or security overrides.
- Invoke **I Know the Secret of the Admiral's "Daughter"** to blackmail your way into high-security areas.

**Fate Point Economy**:
- **Spend on**: Declaring story details ("I know the guy running this checkpoint").
- **Gain from**: Compels on **I Owe My Life to a Junk-Heap Pilot** dragging you into unwanted side jobs.

**Strengths**: Access, information gathering, bypassing obstacles.
**Weaknesses**: Struggles in wilderness or alien environments where rank implies nothing.

---

### Style 3: The Paranoid Survivor (Defensive)

**Core Approach**: You are the first to spot danger and the hardest to kill, prioritizing survival above glory.

**Tactics**:
- Rely on **"Sarge's Sixth Sense"** to prevent ambushes. Always roll Notice.
- Use **Athletics** and **Physique** to stay mobile and absorb hits.
- Create Advantages like *Defensive Position* or *Booby Traps*.
- Invoke **A Soldier's Sixth Sense** to retroactively have prepared for a surprise.

**Fate Point Economy**:
- **Spend on**: Defensive rerolls or soaking massive damage.
- **Gain from**: Compels on **Cynical Ex-Galactic Marine** refusing to trust "obvious" traps or good news.

**Strengths**: survivability, ambush prevention.
**Weaknesses**: Can be reactive rather than proactive.

---

### Style 4: The Aggressive Sergeant (Combat)

**Core Approach**: You use violence of action to overwhelm enemies, terrifying them into submission.

**Tactics**:
- Use **Provoke** (+3) to attack mental stress tracks, making enemies surrender or flee.
- Combine **Shoot** with **Fight** for close-quarters combat ("Gun-Fu").
- Invoke **Flashbacks to the Felinid Uprising** (ironically) to go berserk against non-feline enemies.
- Accept consequences to stay in the fight, showing how tough you are.

**Fate Point Economy**:
- **Spend on**: Powering up attacks to take out minions in one shot.
- **Gain from**: Compels on **Trouble** when your aggression causes collateral damage.

**Strengths**: Mental attacks, intimidation.
**Weaknesses**: High risk of physical consequences.

---

### Universal Tactics (All Styles)

**Aspect Invocation Priority**:
1. **High Concept** (**Cynical Ex-Galactic Marine**): Use for almost any soldiering task.
2. **Phase Aspects**: Use specific history (**Admiral's Secret** or **Junk-Heap Pilot**) for social leverage.
3. **Trouble**: Use only when desperation or trauma fuels your action (risky).

**Compel Management**:
- **Accept**: Compels on **Trouble** (cats/flashbacks) are your main source of Fate Points. Lean into the comedy/tragedy of a hardened soldier scared of a house cat.
- **Refuse**: Do not accept compels that remove your agency in a critical tactical moment unless the Fate Point is worth losing the objective.

**Creating Advantages**:
Use **Contacts** (+4) before the mission to create *Supplies* or *Intel*. During the mission, use **Shoot** (+3) to create *Suppressed* or *Flanked*.

**Stress Management**:
- **Physical Stress**: 3 boxes. You can take a hit, but don't face-tank bosses.
- **Mental Stress**: 3 boxes. You are mentally resilient. Use this to soak fear or intimidation attacks.

**Recommended Starting Style**: Try **Style 1: The Squad Leader**. It leverages your highest skill (Contacts) and unique Stunts most effectively, making you invaluable to any party composition.

**Most Important**: You are the glue that holds the team together. You might be grumpy, but you keep them alive.
